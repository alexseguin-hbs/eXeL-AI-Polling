## 1 · Master of Thought — 333 words

Your r.204 is live and the reading works. Twelve of us played it on your phone and three other sizes: a whole forty-round table each, both training runs, every target taken by hand through TARGET, APPROVE, FIRE. The words under the bullseye, the lit button and the toast said the same thing every time. The clocks are true: five, eight, twelve and sixteen seconds up, three between, ten at the magazine change, three for a hit target to come back, and the train-down clock matched a real watch. I measured them myself. Eighteen groups, forty rounds, four magazines; the cold end is the best screen you own.

Two things are wrong, both the next build. First, the table has an ending and no beginning. You press QUAL 40 and three seconds later a target is up, the magazine already in, nobody said a word. Your own line promises it starts on green; it starts dark. So r.205 gives it the range officer: the range is clear, the firing line is hot, with a ten-round magazine load, make ready, is the line ready, watch your lane, commence firing. The weapon starts empty until you load it, and nothing moves until you press READY. Second, the light is backwards. Green never once lit while a target stood; it lights after the target falls, over empty ground, while red burns when you still must shoot. Green means fire in both training runs. Then the small ones: the round counter can never reach green on a ten-round magazine, and entering too fast gives thirty rounds.

On your phone you will press QUAL 40 and nothing will move. A red light, an empty weapon, the officer's words one at a time. You load. You press READY. The light goes green and the first target rises on that green: green while you may shoot, amber for the last two seconds, red the moment it is gone. The counter green on a full magazine. Everything else stays as it is.

### The build list it wrote, in its order

**1. The range officer's opening on QUAL 40 — six commands on a rolling three-line card (#rsoCard): THE RANGE IS CLEAR · THE FIRING LINE IS HOT · WITH A TEN-ROUND MAGAZINE, LOAD · MAKE READY · IS THE FIRING LINE READY? · WATCH YOUR LANE · COMMENCE FIRING. The weapon starts EMPTY (state.mag rounds 0, n 0) so LOAD means something; two firer presses (RELOAD at LOAD, #btnRangeReset morphing to READY at the ready check); rsoTick(dt) first in rangeTick's qual40 branch as the gate so nothing rises, spends or lapses while the officer speaks; rsoStart() hooked into goRange() only (covers enterTheRange, the mode picker and RESTART), a no-op while state.qaArmed; HOT + the existing RELOAD + READY decision rows mirroring the COLD row; ev('QUAL','P1','START') bracketing the existing DONE.**

*Why.* His 04:34 order, in his own words. The table has a full ceremony at the end (CEASE FIRE / UNLOAD / SHOW CLEAR / score / THE LINE IS CLEAR / RANGE IS COLD) and total silence at the start: I measured entry as lamp dark, 'NEXT 3 S', magazine already seated at MAG 1/4 · 10 RDS, and the first target up 3 s later with no command and no row on the record. Grep of the served bytes returns ZERO occurrences of LOAD AND MAKE READY, MAKE READY, IS THE LINE READY, WATCH YOUR LANE, COMMENCE FIRING or RANGE IS HOT. And magReload('MANUAL') at entry returns false with 'MAGAZINE FULL · 10 RDS' — the very first command he named is one the deck refuses today, which is why the weapon must start empty. TRAIN DOWN right beside it already has exactly this arm-then-START gate (lamp R, word START, button START, zero up — measured), so this is wiring an existing pattern, not new machinery.

*Gate.* RSO_OPENS_COLD_AND_EMPTY (card reads THE RANGE IS CLEAR, lamp R, MAG 0/4 · 0 RDS, RELOAD lit, no plate up, qualR 0) · RSO_NOTHING_RUNS_BEFORE_COMMENCE (60 s of rangeTick inside the opening raises no target, spends no round, books no lapse) · RSO_LOAD_IS_THE_FIRERS_PRESS (RELOAD refused BEFORE_THE_COMMAND at CLEAR/HOT, accepted at LOAD) · RSO_READY_IS_THE_FIRERS_PRESS (the button reads READY only at ASK; without the press the table never starts) · RSO_SIX_COMMANDS_IN_ORDER (card never more than three lines, last lamp G) · RSO_MIRRORS_THE_COLD_END (LOAD · MAKE READY · FIRE at the open, CEASE FIRE · UNLOAD · SHOW CLEAR at the close — the six TC 3-22.9 weapons commands, each exactly once across both cards). All six asserted at 428×926, 390×844, 375×667 and 844×390 with #rsoCard checked against hudKeepOut() for zero overlap; add 'rsoCard' AND 'qualOver' (missing today) to the keep-out id list.

**2. The lamp leads the window instead of trailing it. Rewrite qualLamp(R): during 'up' return 'G' (the range is hot) — held through the window, amber for its last two seconds; during a gap or phase gap return 'R' at gap−2, 'Y' at gap−1, so red and yellow come BEFORE the targets rise and green arrives as they stand. The opening hands over by setting rangeRun(lane).t = ENG_GAP_S while the lamp is green, so engagement 1 rises ON the green and his own sentence 'STARTS ON GREEN' becomes literally true. Remove the dark 'O' state from a hot QUAL table entirely.**

*Why.* Twelve of twelve Masters and all three specialists found this independently, and I verified it myself on the live bytes: over 1,268 samples at 428×926, GREEN with a target standing = 0, GREEN with nothing up = 143, RED with a target up = 142, YELLOW with a target up = 138, DARK with a target up = 845. Source confirms it — qualLamp() (play.html:961) returns 'O' until sec−2, 'R' at sec−2, 'Y' at sec−1 and 'G' only once the window has CLOSED. The deck's own comment at line 955 says 'RED wait · YELLOW ready · GREEN fire' and modeSentence() (:934) promises 'STARTS ON GREEN'; both training modes already use the correct grammar, so the one mode carrying the Army's name teaches the opposite. Red currently carries four meanings on one lane.

*Gate.* QUAL_LAMP_LEADS_THE_WINDOW — across a full simulated table no tick has lamp G with zero targets up and no tick has a target up with lamp R or O · RSO_FIRST_EXPOSURE_ON_THE_GREEN — engagement 1 rises within 0.1 s of the lamp going G. CRITICAL: the existing QA row START_LIGHT_SEQUENCE (play.html:4580) asserts the INVERTED order 'O → R → Y → G' trailing each window — the gate currently defends the defect. Rewrite that expectation to the leading order and name the edit explicitly in the release note (it is a QA expectation, not a shipped ledger block).

**3. The word under the lamp comes back in QUAL 40. Delete the blanking tail of paintTimer() (play.html:976) that forces #startWord to '' whenever the mode is qual40; give the timer its own line beneath the word. Drive the lamp's aria-label from the state rather than from lightWord(): 'range green, fire, 12 seconds' / 'range amber, 2 seconds left' / 'range red, no target' / 'range red, cease fire, range is cold'. During the opening the card's own commands ARE the word — do not blank it there either.**

*Why.* Measured: #startWord held '' in every one of 1,268 samples of a QUAL table, and the aria-label read 'start light ' at dark. So the one mode where the light is timing-critical is the only mode with no word to read it by, and a colour-blind player has a countdown number and nothing else. Worse, the aria-label is still fed by lightWord() even though the visible word is blanked, so a screen-reader user is told 'start light WAIT' with a target up and two seconds left, and 'start light FIRE' over empty ground — the opposite of the truth at every moment. The existing QA row START_LIGHT_WORDS asserts R:WAIT Y:READY G:FIRE but reads it OUTSIDE qual40, so it stays green while the mode that needs it has none.

*Gate.* Extend START_LIGHT_WORDS to read inside qual40 as well, which is where it was always meant to apply; add an assertion that the spoken aria-label equals the colour's meaning in every state (hot window, last seconds, gap, phase gap, cold end).

**4. The round counter's colour rule scales to the magazine in the weapon. In hudMag(), replace the fixed 30-round bands (green ≥ 20 · amber 6–19 · red ≤ 5) with bands derived from magCap(): green above two thirds of cap, amber below, red at 5 or under — so a full magazine is green at 10 the way it is at 30, and his rule reads the same in both.**

*Why.* magCap() is 10 in QUAL 40 but hudMag()'s rule is the 30-round rule, so a brand-new FULL qual magazine already paints amber — I measured #magRounds at rgb(240,160,32) with 10 of 10 rounds at entry — and from the sixth round it paints red. Green never appears once in a whole forty-round table, on any of the four magazines. The counter's only two states for the entire qualification are warning and danger, which is the one colour language on the glass the shooter cannot learn to trust. The rule and the cap live in two different places, which is why this needs a gate and not just a line.

*Gate.* A new row that steps the count 10→0 at cap 10 and 30→0 at cap 30 and asserts green at full, amber in the middle band and red at ≤5 in BOTH, so the rule and the cap can never drift apart again.

**5. The magazine cap cannot be stolen by the deck's own self-test. Make the register save and restore the player's rangeMode and state.mag around its range rows, or refuse to run those rows once a lane is armed.**

*Why.* Reproduced and confirmed on the live bytes: entering the range before the register finishes (a human-speed tap) leaves state.mag.cap = 30 while state.rangeMode is 'qual40' — measured cap 10 at 0.01 s, cap 30 by 1.5 s, and STILL 30 at 5.2 s, so it sticks for the whole table. That is 120 rounds issued for a 40-round table, thirty bars in his magazine picture, and the phase-gap RELOAD reduced to decoration because he never runs dry. The register's last range rows do state.rangeMode='bounce'; rangeReset() → magLoad('RESET') at cap 30 and never put the player's lane back. The table is therefore right or wrong depending on how fast he taps — the single worst kind of defect to leave in a demo he hands to someone else.

*Gate.* A row that enters qual40 while the register is still running and asserts cap 10, four magazines and 40 rounds; plus an assertion that no QA row leaves state.rangeMode or state.mag changed from what it found.

**6. The cold line stops cueing FIRE. Route #phDes to the existing cold string (noLockMsg's 'CEASE FIRE · QUALIFICATION COMPLETE · PRESS RESTART', play.html:2129) when state.qualDone, and clear the step toast at CEASE FIRE so the last shot's recital does not outlive the cease fire.**

*Why.* After the 40th round the glass simultaneously says CEASE FIRE / UNLOAD / SHOW CLEAR / RANGE IS COLD on the overlay and 'TARGET FIRST' (or 'LAST · 250 M · DOWN') in the recital, with the bottom toast still reading 'HIT · 250 M · TARGET · APPROVE · FIRE'. The line r.204 made the authority — the one under the bullseye he just called 'way better' — is the one line that contradicts the cold range. Four Masters caught it independently (Asar, Pangu, Enlil, the UX specialist).

*Gate.* Extend the existing QUAL_ENDS_COLD row to assert the recital and the toast as well as the overlay, the lamp and the RELOAD label — one state, every voice.

**7. An empty magazine is named as an empty magazine. Hoist the magHasRound() guard (play.html:2596) ABOVE the NO_RED_BOX guard (:2584) in fireN; add a rounds<=0 branch to nextStep()/#phDes reading 'EMPTY · PRESS RELOAD' in every state, marked or not; make the TARGET and APPROVE toasts defer to the reload state.**

*Why.* With a dry weapon and nothing marked, the deck says 'NO RED BOX · MARK A TARGET FIRST' and sends him to mark and approve a target that can never be fired — the refusal names the wrong cause because the guards are in the wrong order. With a target marked, two of the three voices still say PRESS FIRE while the recital correctly says PRESS RELOAD. The word RELOAD appears nowhere in the recital or the toast; the only cue is a pulse on a 60×75 button in the top-left corner. This is the one place r.204's 'the words and the lit button read one state' promise breaks.

*Gate.* A row that empties the magazine in both the marked and unmarked state and asserts all three voices — recital, toast and refusal — name RELOAD.

**8. The stance change gets a word and its warning. Make qualPose() show the NEXT pose during a within-phase gap, as the 10 s phase gaps already do, and toast the change in that gap: the TACTICAL STANDING → UNSUPPORTED PRONE move between engagement 1 and 2 is announced before engagement 2's clock starts.**

*Why.* POSE_BY_ENG moves the shooter from standing to prone INSIDE phase 1, in an ordinary 3 s gap, with no command and only the little stance picture changing — and because qualPose() during a within-phase gap still shows the pose of the engagement that just ended, the card flips only at the instant engagement 2 is already up and its 5 s clock is running. The single biggest position change on the table gets zero warning. Krishna, Sofia and Thor each found it separately; the 10 s phase gaps already do it correctly, so this is making one path match the other.

*Gate.* A row asserting that during every gap the pose card shows the COMING engagement's pose, and that a within-phase pose change raises a toast in its gap.

**9. The TRAIN DOWN clock stops at the shot. Call downStop() when the last plate's lifePct reaches 0 — the hit — instead of when its fall animation finishes (rangeTick's training branch currently treats a falling plate as anyUp and returns early); let the fall play on afterwards. Round downClockWord() to the nearest tenth with a 1e-6 epsilon instead of Math.floor(), or show hundredths.**

*Why.* Measured: the last plate killed at state.downT = 2.500 and the clock stopped at 3.000 — a systematic +0.500 s over-read on every timed run, because the 1/2.4 = 0.4167 s fall is counted. On top of that, floor() with no epsilon turns an internal 2.9999999999999996 s into a displayed 0:02.9. Net +0.32 to +0.42 s. He asked that the times be accurate per real tests, and a shot timer stops at the shot. (Separately noted, not fixed here: rangeDt() caps a step at 0.25 s, so one janky frame silently adds a quarter second.)

*Gate.* A row that kills the last plate at a known downT and asserts the recorded time is within one frame of it, plus a truncation case asserting an internal 2.99999 s does not display 0:02.9.

**10. Say DECLARED where it is declared. Label EXPOSURE_BY_COUNT, ENG_GAP_S and RETURN_S in the code and in any player-facing text as the operator's rule, not as TC 3-20.40. Raise two things with him as questions, not changes: (a) one public secondary page gives the first 50 m target 3 seconds where the deck gives 5; (b) the real EXPERT rule appears to require 36+ hits AND at least one hit on one of the five 300 m exposures — the deck has exactly those five, so qualBadge() could implement it with no new state. Also note RETURN_S is 3 s from DOWN but 3.43 s from the shot (I measured 0.398 fall / 3.033 return / 3.431 total) — either count it from the hit or say 3.5 s.**

*Why.* He ordered the times double-checked against real tests, and every measured time matches its declared constant to within 36 ms — 5.018 / 5.013 / 5.009 / 12.014 / 16.015 s, gaps 3.000–3.026 s, phase gap 10.036 s on my own run. What cannot be verified is the declaration itself: TC 3-20.40's Table VI exposure times are not published in any source reachable here — the 2018 reporting explicitly says the final product differs from the draft 'only in the exposure times' and never lists them. What IS corroborated is 40 rounds, four 10-round magazines, up to four targets at once, the 6/7/8/8/6/5 range distribution, the badge cuts 36/30/23 and the transition clock starting only after the exposure ends. Honesty about which numbers are his and which are the Army's costs nothing and protects the deck.

*Gate.* The existing QUAL_EXPOSURE_BY_COUNT and IWQ_PROGRAM_IS_40 rows carry the DECLARED label in their message text; if he approves the 300 m EXPERT rule, a row that drops a 40-hit run with zero 300 m hits to SHARPSHOOTER.

## 2 · UX usability test — Drone-2525 "The Range" r.204 (four cold personas, 428×926 / 390×844 / 844×390 / 375×667)

**Verdict.** The three-step gate is the best thing on the glass and needs no rework: 4 taps and 1.75 s to a first hit in TRAIN UP, 6 taps and 4.67 s in QUAL 40, and at every moment the lit button, the recital (#phDes) and the toast name the same next step. r.204's "read the target under the bullseye" genuinely lands — every one of my out-of-order presses was refused with a sentence that named the real next action ("NO RED BOX · MARK A TARGET FIRST", "AMBER · PRESS APPROVE FIRST"). The times are honest: over 146 s of wall clock the 5/8/12/16 s windows, the 3 s engagement gaps and the 10 s phase gaps all held to within 30 ms, 18 engagements, 40 rounds, phases at exactly 10/20/30. What fails is everything AROUND the gate. The stop light does not mean what a stop light means: in QUAL 40 GREEN appeared 11 times and never once with a target standing, while RED appeared 11 times and every single time with a target standing — the lamp is exactly inverted against the range. In TRAIN UP it reads GREEN/"FIRE" from the instant you land, before anything is targeted, while FIRE is refused. QUAL 40 begins with no range commands at all — you pick a mode and the Army's qualification table is already running — even though TRAIN DOWN right beside it already has the exact arm-then-START ceremony the operator is asking for, and the cold end already recites the full CEASE FIRE / UNLOAD / SHOW CLEAR / RANGE IS COLD script. And a third of the qualification is invisible: 5 of 15 targets rose completely off the left edge of the glass while the strip said "1 UP" and no arrow pointed anywhere. Not ready for a cold soldier or a cold civilian; the demo survives only because the operator knows where to look.

- **BLOCKING — QUAL 40's stop light is exactly inverted — green only ever shows after the target is down**
  - *Evidence.* 40 Hz trace of 146 s of real wall clock (qual-trace.json, 57 transitions, 11 engagements): GREEN appeared 11 times, with a target standing 0 of 11 times; RED appeared 11 times, with a target standing 11 of 11 times; YELLOW 11 times, all 11 with a target standing. Every GREEN sample had phase='gap' and zero targets up. Per-state capture confirms the aria wording: at RED, up=1, phDes='50 M RIGHT · PRESS TARGET', aria='start light WAIT'; at GREEN, up=0, phDes='TARGET FIRST', aria='start light FIRE'. So the lamp says WAIT while a target is exposed and you must shoot, and FIRE one second after it has fallen and no shot is possible.
  - *Fix.* Rewrite qualLamp (:961) to range semantics: GREEN for the whole open window, YELLOW for the last 2 s, RED from the instant the window shuts through the gap. Gate it with a test asserting no GREEN sample coincides with zero targets up and no RED sample with a target up.

- **BLOCKING — QUAL 40 starts with no range commands — the table is already running the moment you pick the mode**
  - *Evidence.* Selecting QUAL 40 from the mode picker lands on: light dark, no word, timer already counting 'NEXT 3 S', #btnRangeReset reading 'RESTART' and not pulsing. Nothing is said before live fire. Grep of the whole deck returns 0 occurrences of 'MAKE READY', 'WATCH YOUR LANE', 'COMMENCE', 'FIRERS' and 'SAFETY'. The contrast is internal: picking TRAIN DOWN gives light=R, word='START', button text='START' with call=true and every target down, and pressing it gives light=G, word='TIME', 11 targets up. The cold END of QUAL 40 is already a complete RSO ceremony (CEASE FIRE / UNLOAD / SHOW CLEAR / n OF 40 / THE LINE IS CLEAR / RANGE IS COLD · PRESS RESTART). Only the hot start is missing, in the one mode that is a real Army qualification table.
  - *Fix.* Mirror downArm/downStart (:917-918) and paintQualOver (:938): hold the table on mode entry with every target down and the lamp red, make the button read START, and recite WITH A 10-ROUND MAGAZINE, LOAD · LOAD AND MAKE READY · IS THE LINE READY · THE RANGE IS HOT · WATCH YOUR LANE · COMMENCE FIRING. Recite CEASE FIRE · CHANGE MAGAZINES · LOAD AND MAKE READY at each 10 s phase gap.

- **BLOCKING — A third of the QUAL targets rise completely off the left edge of the glass, with no cue**
  - *Evidence.* Measured plateRect for every target as its engagement rose: 5 of 15 were off-glass at rise (eng 3 C-150L at cx=-23; eng 4 C-50L at cx=-124; eng 5 C-150L cx=-23 and C-250 cx=-2; eng 6 C-100L cx=-24). Engagements 3 and 6 are single-target, so their entire 5 s window shows an empty screen. Captured at engagement 3: the strip reads 'PHASE 1 · ENG 3/18 · 1 UP · 5 S · HIT 0/2 · NOT ENGAGED 2', the recital reads 'TARGET FIRST', all three lamps are dark, all three buttons are dark, and a scan for any directional cue found none. The deck knows a target is up, tells the user to target, and shows nothing to target and no direction to look.
  - *Fix.* Slew the head onto the rising engagement automatically, or paint an edge arrow with the range ('◀ 150 M LEFT') for any target that is up and off-screen.

- **BLOCKING — An empty magazine is misdiagnosed as a missing red box, because fireN checks the red box before the rounds**
  - *Evidence.* In fireN the NO_RED_BOX refusal is at play.html:2584 and the EMPTY_MAGAZINE check at :2596, so the red-box branch always wins when nothing is marked. Reproduced honestly by firing all 10 rounds of magazine 1 through the real gate, then waiting for a fresh unmarked target: phDes read 'LAST · 300 M · DOWN', all three buttons dark, and tapping FIRE returned 'NO RED BOX · MARK A TARGET FIRST'. The word RELOAD appears nowhere in the recital or the toast; the only cue is the pulse class on the 60×75 RELOAD button at the top-left corner. The user is sent to mark and approve a target that can never be fired.
  - *Fix.* Hoist the magHasRound() guard above the red-box guard in fireN, and add a rounds<=0 branch to the recital so it reads 'EMPTY · PRESS RELOAD' whether or not a target is marked.

- **BLOCKING — TRAIN UP reads GREEN / "FIRE" before anything is targeted, while FIRE is refused**
  - *Evidence.* On landing from THE RANGE the light is G with the word 'FIRE' and the lit button is TARGET. Pressing FIRE at that moment is refused with 'NO RED BOX · MARK A TARGET FIRST'; pressing APPROVE is refused with 'NO AMBER · MARK A TARGET FIRST'. The light stayed G/'FIRE' through every step of the gate, including both refusals. Across the three modes the same green lamp carries three different meanings — TRAIN UP green = a target stands (but the gate may refuse), TRAIN DOWN green = the clock is running, QUAL 40 green = the window just closed — and none of the three means 'you may fire now'.
  - *Fix.* In startLight's training branch (:955) require that firing would actually be permitted before returning G, so green never shows while the gate refuses the shot.

- **BLOCKING — The magazine never follows the phase — a whole 40-round table reads "MAG 1/4 · 10 RDS"**
  - *Evidence.* Drove a complete table with every target lapsed: phase 1 begins at t=2.8 s, phase 2 at 68.1 s, phase 3 at 131.5 s, phase 4 at 190.8 s — correctly at 10, 20 and 30 scored rounds — yet the magazine read 1/4 with 10 rounds at every boundary and at the end (magN=1, magRounds=10, magDom='10', label 'MAG 1/4 · 10 RDS') while qualR reached 40. A lapsed target scores a round but never spends one, so the table count and the magazine count diverge and are never reconciled on the glass. magLoad('PHASE') exists for exactly this and is not called at the phase boundary. The cold-end screenshot shows 'MAG 1/4 · 10 RDS' beside '0 OF 40'.
  - *Fix.* Call magLoad('PHASE') at each phase gap so the glass reads MAG 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, and show the table's spent-round count beside the magazine count so the two never silently disagree.

- **NOTE — The colour-blind word under the lamp is blank for the whole of QUAL 40**
  - *Evidence.* paintCues calls paintLight — which writes WAIT / READY / FIRE into #startWord — and then calls paintTimer, whose tail blanks #startWord whenever the mode is qual40. Captured at all four lamp states in QUAL 40: domWord was "" every time, while the aria-label still carried 'start light WAIT' / 'READY' / 'FIRE'. A screen-reader user is told; a sighted colour-blind user is left with lamp position alone, in the one mode where the lamp is timing-critical.
  - *Fix.* Drop the blanking condition at the end of paintTimer (:976) and give the timer its own line beneath the word.

- **NOTE — The recital still says "TARGET FIRST" while the range is cold**
  - *Evidence.* At the cold end the overlay reads CEASE FIRE / UNLOAD / SHOW CLEAR / THE LINE IS CLEAR / RANGE IS COLD, the lamp is red with 'CEASE FIRE · COLD', RELOAD is locked and relabelled UNLOAD — and #phDes at the top-left still reads 'TARGET FIRST'. The one line r.204 made the authority is the one line that contradicts the cold line.
  - *Fix.* Route #phDes to the existing noLockMsg cold string 'CEASE FIRE · QUALIFICATION COMPLETE · PRESS RESTART' when state.qualDone.

- **NOTE — Toast and recital give opposite instructions on a dry magazine**
  - *Evidence.* With the magazine empty and a target marked, #phDes correctly reads 'RED · T1 · 100 M LEFT · PRESS RELOAD', but tapping TARGET toasts 'RED BOX · 100 M LEFT · PRESS FIRE' and tapping APPROVE toasts 'RED BOX · 100 M LEFT · APPROVED BY YOU · NOW PRESS FIRE'. Only the third tap, on FIRE itself, says 'EMPTY · PRESS RELOAD'. Two of the deck's three voices send the user to a button that will refuse.
  - *Fix.* Make the TARGET and APPROVE toasts defer to the reload state when rounds<=0.

- **NOTE — The three gate buttons are under the minimum touch height in portrait**
  - *Evidence.* Measured at all four viewports: TARGET / APPROVE / FIRE are 72×40 at 428×926, 390×844 and 375×667, and 64–71×44 only in landscape at 844×390. #rngMode (95×32), #lanePick (77×32) and #btnRangeReset (56×32) are 32 px tall at every size. No horizontal overflow at any viewport and nothing scrolls off, so the layout is otherwise sound on a phone.
  - *Fix.* Raise the three gate buttons to 44 px tall in portrait, matching what landscape already does, and take the 32 px control row to 44.

- **NOTE — A judge who watches before touching finds the demo already scored against them**
  - *Evidence.* Watching QUAL 40 for 60 s without touching the glass: 'PHASE 1 · ENG 5/18 · MAG CHANGE · HIT 0/10 · NOT ENGAGED 10', ten rounds scored, zero hits. At 75 s it reads 'NOT ENGAGED 11'. This is faithful to the Army table — an unengaged target costs the round — but to a cold viewer in a two-minute demo the first thing the deck says is that they have failed, with no prior invitation to begin. The missing hot start (finding 2) is what makes this read as a broken demo rather than as a qualification already under way.
  - *Fix.* The START gate from finding 2 removes this entirely: nothing scores until the user presses START.

- **NOTE — The cold intro offers three equal buttons and no hint which one is the demo**
  - *Evidence.* The first screen is 'DRONE-2525 / TODAY / Today we protect the Capital of Texas.' with CONTINUE, SKIP TO SELECT and THE RANGE rendered as three identically weighted green outline buttons on an otherwise empty black screen; the operator's own path is the third and rightmost. Nothing distinguishes it. Once pressed, the path is excellent — 4 taps and 1.75 s to a first hit in TRAIN UP, 6 taps and 4.67 s in QUAL 40, 9 taps and 12.14 s for the next three targets — so the only hesitation on this path is the choice itself.
  - *Fix.* Give THE RANGE the primary weight (filled, first position) and leave the other two as secondary text buttons.
