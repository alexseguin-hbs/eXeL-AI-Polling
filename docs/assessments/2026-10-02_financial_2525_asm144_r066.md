## The twelve overviews (111 words each)

### Aset

Trust begins right there, and everything else rests safely upon it.

### Asar

Asar weighs the final synthesis: does the system tell one coherent story? Twelve scenarios say yes. Income arrives as a rate, spending leaves as a rate or a lump, and the gap between them is Available, which may now dip below zero when you pay ahead, because the month's income still covers it. The Accrual Rate then shows what remains once that advance is spread over the rest of the month, so a bold payment early reads honestly as a slower month later. Nothing is hidden or double counted. His summary is: one ledger, one rate, one picture, and every figure on the glass traces back to entries you can replay.
