## Headline (Master of Thought)

The engine of the case is sound and honest: human authority is explicit, nothing current is re-sold as new, no price is claimed before a buyer verifies minutes. Three defects make the v0.9 deck unfit for the founder as it stands, and all twelve lenses found the same three: **(1)** the deck argues the stance the operator retired — "layer above, replace none" on S1 / S6 / S8 / S13 / S18 and in the master question — while the future state is ONE platform carrying every aspect of the existing NBA; **(2)** the Innovation Pod template prints dollars the deck disowns (header COGS / MSRP / margin, S2 / S3 return profile, S10 financials by year, derived from the row's placeholder NRE / revenue) — an evidence-law breach in the artefact itself; **(3)** three benchmarks dilute conviction — a founder cannot say the platform's job in one breath, and an investor cannot size a company that sits "above" three others.

## Decisions folded into the v1.0 ONE-PLATFORM edition

1. **Platform benchmark = Everbridge 360 AI / Bridge** (11 of 12 lenses; Enlil argued PagerDuty for buildability). 4. **Human authority scales with the platform:** every outbound action — an alert, a task, a release — carries its decision right, a named approver and a timestamp; the machine never sends on its own (R-CORE Manual cadence by default, Semi-Automated earned per capability). "AI appears to command" is rated Medium with a mechanical control, not Low.
5. **Unpriced by rule, mechanically:** the PRJ-34 row is typed `unpriced`; the Pod hides the derived money (header line, S2 / S3 return profile, S10 grid) and prints "unpriced — evidence law" in its place; a gate holds it.
6. **One thesis on every slide:** S1 / S6 / S8 / S13 / S18 and the master question are rewritten to the single-platform stance; "becomes Everbridge-lite" is reframed from a fence into the ladder (absorb only by qualification). The CONFIRM line on the commercial spine moves to speaker notes.
7. **The citizen enters the record** (Christo · Sofia · Enki · Aset): the person who receives the alert is a persona (S7) and a source of truth (CASPER-shaped intake, S4); stakeholder posture in every language (S15); a degraded mode where a completed approval is never lost (S13); succession of decision rights and step-up identity for approvers (S6); privilege-aware record fields (S13); a second clock for slow-burn crises (S15).
8. **Evidence that can fail** (Thoth · Odin): the S16 A/B is pre-registered with a hypothesis, a null and a stop rule, run order counterbalanced, an independent timekeeper; every S14 clock is a state transition the platform itself logs; G3 needs three institutions per segment or S16 is labelled pilot; the pre-registered EDU tabletop runs before any external use.
