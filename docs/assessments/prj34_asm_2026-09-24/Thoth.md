## 6 · The single platform

**Benchmark NBA: Everbridge 360 AI / Bridge.** Of the three, it is the only one that already spans the whole surface — risk intelligence, emergency communications, incident-response workflow, coordinated action, continuity planning, dependency context and AI-assisted guidance — so it has the most "aspects" to carry inside one platform; it sits in two of the three current stacks (EDU alongside Rave, FOOD as primary, per the narrative §4); and its AI-assisted guidance is the exact contest where human-authority-by-design can be measured, not asserted. Rave is a notification subset bound to one sector; PagerDuty is the technical half of one segment. One benchmark gives one metric family that compares across segments, which the three-benchmark design (S1, S16) cannot.

**Strongest value proposition, one sentence:** CrisisCommand.ai is the one platform where an organisation's leaders see the truth, decide with named authority, alert and act, and prove afterwards what was known, decided and done — with humans owning every consequence.

**"Every aspect of the existing NBA inside CrisisCommand.ai" at three horizons:**
- **2027** — In the first proof segment, alerting, status and help reporting, response workflow and the continuity checklist run inside CrisisCommand.ai under the governed leadership layer; the NBA becomes the A arm of the A/B, not a partner, and one clock family is logged by the platform itself.
- **2030** — The full Everbridge-class critical-event surface (risk intelligence, communications, response, continuity, dependency context) plus decision rights, approval state, ownership, evidence and outcome-linked replay, across EDU, FOOD and TECH; technical incident state still arrives by pointer, and replay compares outcomes across events and institutions.
- **2525** — The platform is a civilisation nervous system for institutions: memory of every decision and its outcome across generations, autonomy earned per capability by measured qualification, and the covenant intact — humanity decides, technology assists, trust is proven each cycle.

Fix these and the story is falsifiable, which is the only kind investors should fund. Then go further: make CrisisCommand.ai the single platform that alerts, coordinates, decides and remembers, benchmarked against Everbridge, measured by one clock family, gated by an eleven-percent improvement rule before any capability is called proven. Humans approve every consequence. Numbers decide gates.
