## 6 · The single platform — which ONE NBA should be the platform benchmark and why (Rave / Everbridge 360 AI–Bridge / PagerDuty / other); the strongest value proposition in ONE sentence; what "every aspect of the existing NBA inside CrisisCommand.ai" means at the 2027 / 2030 / 2525 horizons (three lines)
Benchmark: Everbridge 360 AI / Bridge. Four reasons from this lens. First, it already spans two of the three segments: the narrative names Rave / Everbridge as the default EDU stack and Everbridge / CEM as the FOOD stack, so one benchmark covers the first two proofs. Second, it is the incumbent already moving into CrisisCommand's whitespace (AI-assisted guidance, locked plans, preparedness-to-response feedback); the NBA most likely to absorb CrisisCommand is the one CrisisCommand must absorb first. Third, its job is software and enterprise-wide, risk intelligence, communications, response workflow, continuity, improvement, and therefore absorbable; Rave's job rests on carrier, 911 and campus hardware rails, and PagerDuty's job rests on a developer ecosystem and a restoration clock the deck rightly refuses to claim. Fourth, TECH still works: PagerDuty becomes a structured input to the platform, and the executive half of the technical incident, the part PagerDuty leaves manual, is CrisisCommand's from day one.

Strongest value proposition, one sentence: CrisisCommand.ai is the one platform where a crisis is sensed, judged, approved, executed, communicated and remembered, and every decision carries who had the authority, why it was made and what happened next.

2027: one login and one record; the governed leadership state (decision rights, approval, ownership, acknowledgement, evidence, replay) ships natively, and the critical-event basics a buyer expects from Everbridge (alerting, incident workflow, continuity plan, risk feed) are inside the same platform through built-in or partnered rails, so a president or a COO never leaves it during an event.
2030: the whole critical-event job is native, risk intelligence, multi-channel communication, response workflows, continuity, simulation and multi-event learning, incumbents are optional channels rather than systems of record, and the enterprise decision graph is the organization's crisis memory that regulators, boards and insurers read directly.
2525: the memory outlives the people; institutions in education, food and technology, then every domain, learn from each other's replays under human authority through the ladder pilot → replay → qualify → certify → adopt → educate → expand, so a crisis anywhere teaches leadership everywhere, and nothing is decided by the machine alone.
