## 6 · The single platform — which ONE NBA should be the platform benchmark and why (Rave / Everbridge 360 AI–Bridge / PagerDuty / other); the strongest value proposition in ONE sentence; what "every aspect of the existing NBA inside CrisisCommand.ai" means at the 2027 / 2030 / 2525 horizons (three lines)
Benchmark: **Everbridge 360 AI / Bridge.** It is the only one of the three that already spans the whole event — risk intelligence, communications, incident workflow, coordinated action, continuity and learning — so "every aspect of the existing NBA inside one platform" has one concrete, finite definition; Rave is a subset (notification and campus safety, owned by a defence-scale incumbent whose retaliation lever is life-safety liability) and PagerDuty is a different buyer and a deep integration moat that would pull the company into engineering tooling. Everbridge already claims AI-assisted guidance, which is exactly where authority drifts and exactly where CrisisCommand's visible human approval is the difference a general counsel will pay for; and it is present in the EDU and FOOD stacks today, so the same benchmark serves two of three proofs. The risk of choosing the category king is bundling and price; the control is the buyer door (S7) and the record the king does not hold.
Value proposition: **CrisisCommand.ai is the one crisis platform where an organization sees the facts, decides under named human authority, acts, tells every stakeholder one approved posture, and keeps the proof — so it never needs a second system to respond or to show it responded well.**
- 2027 — one pane, not yet one engine: every Everbridge-class function is reachable from inside CrisisCommand by pointer and status, the governance record is native, and every action carries a named approver; nothing life-critical runs natively yet.
- 2030 — one engine, earned function by function: communications, workflow, continuity and risk intelligence run natively where each has beaten the benchmark on the same pre-registered clock, with alerting last and certified; the incumbent remains only where a customer chooses to keep it.
- 2525 — one institutional memory for humanity: any organization, in any language, decides under pressure with the replay of every prior decision behind it, humans still approve and own consequences, and the platform is the nervous system of trust under pressure rather than a vendor.
