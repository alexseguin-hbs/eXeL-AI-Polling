## 6 · The single platform — which ONE NBA should be the platform benchmark and why (Rave / Everbridge 360 AI–Bridge / PagerDuty / other); the strongest value proposition in ONE sentence; what "every aspect of the existing NBA inside CrisisCommand.ai" means at the 2027 / 2030 / 2525 horizons (three lines)
Benchmark: Everbridge 360 AI–Bridge. Three reasons from the record. First, it is the only NBA whose job already spans the whole loop the deck draws on S6 — risk intelligence, emergency communication, incident workflow, coordinated action, continuity planning and preparedness-to-response learning — so carrying "every aspect" of it inside CrisisCommand yields a complete critical-event platform, not a feature. Second, the narrative (§ 4 and § 5) places Everbridge in the EDU stack beside Rave and in the FOOD stack, and names it the one competitor that already claims coordination, locked plans and next-step guidance; it is therefore the NBA whose overlap most threatens the S8 whitespace unless it lives inside. Third, it keeps the deck consistent: EDU stays first proof (campus notification is the same job under either name), FOOD is its home ground, and PagerDuty's technical feed and Rave's campus safety feed become absorbed segment capabilities rather than rival stories. Rave is a subset of that job; PagerDuty is the technical half only.
Value proposition (one sentence): CrisisCommand.ai is the one platform where leaders see the truth, decide with named authority, reach and mobilize everyone who must act, prove each action was done, and carry what was learned into the next crisis — humanity deciding, technology assisting, trust proven on the record.
2027 — every aspect of Everbridge 360 AI–Bridge that a leadership team touches during an event runs inside CrisisCommand (risk intake, mass communication, incident workflow, continuity plan state), each step carrying a decision right, an approver, an owner, an acknowledgement and evidence; EDU first proof on the existing Pro Team → Pro Enterprise spine; alerts sent by a person, never by the system.
2030 — one platform across EDU, FOOD and TECH with the technical incident feed (PagerDuty's job) and the campus safety feed (Rave's job) native; the enterprise decision graph replays across events, teams and institutions; every capability qualified before it scales and autonomy earned per capability, never granted.
2525 — the platform is a community's crisis nervous system: anyone, in any language, can see what is known, answer back, watch a named human decide, see the action proven and learn from the replay; the record outlives the people, the vendors and the systems that made it.
