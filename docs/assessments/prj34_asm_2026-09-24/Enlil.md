## 6 · The single platform
**Benchmark: PagerDuty (TECH).** From a builder's seat it is the only NBA whose whole job can be carried inside CrisisCommand with what the company has: its surface is software only and publicly documented — on-call schedules, escalation policies, event routing, service and business-service status, status pages, stakeholder updates, postmortems, integrations — with no carrier contracts, no public-safety certification, no panic-button hardware and no risk-intelligence feed to license. Rave's job needs carrier-grade notification and campus public-safety integration; Everbridge 360 is the widest surface in the market and the worst thing to copy whole. CrisisCommand's TECH edition is already live, its buyer (CISO / CIO / COO) can judge parity, its postmortem is a primitive of the replay CrisisCommand already claims, and the deck itself names TECH the scale-potential segment. One warning: the deck never claims the platform shortens outage duration; the parity claim is about the crisis team's job, the causality claim stays unproven until measured.

**Strongest value proposition (one sentence):** CrisisCommand is the one platform where a technical incident is detected, mobilized and resolved and where leadership approves what it means, who owns the response, what every stakeholder hears and what the organization learns — one record from first alert to last lesson, with a human's name on every decision.

- **2027** — Every job a crisis team asks PagerDuty for (on-call, escalation, routing, status page, stakeholder updates, postmortem) runs natively inside CrisisCommand beside the governed leadership layer; an existing PagerDuty account can be connected but is not required; every outbound action carries its approver.
- **2030** — The same platform carries the FOOD and EDU jobs — recall and quality intake, campus and community notification — with the carrier-grade delivery layer bought or partnered and named as such; multi-event learning across instances; AI proposes, humans commit.
- **2525** — The platform is institutional memory: every crisis an organization faced, decided and learned from, replayable across generations and owned by the people it served; humanity decides, technology assists, trust is proven on the record.
