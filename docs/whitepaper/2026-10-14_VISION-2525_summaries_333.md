**The count is checkable** — a word is a whitespace-separated token containing a letter or a digit, so a standalone
`—` or `·` is punctuation and not a word. Verify either summary with:

```
python3 - <<'EOF'
import re
s = open('docs/whitepaper/2026-10-14_VISION-2525_summaries_333.md').read()
# the two summaries are the prose between an anchored "## " heading and the next anchored "### " heading
secs = re.split(r'(?m)^(?=#{2,3} )', s)
def count(txt):
    return len([w for w in txt.split() if re.search(r'[A-Za-z0-9]', w)])
for sec in secs:
    if sec.startswith('## Vision') and 'sections' in sec.split('\n')[0]:
        print(sec.split('\n')[0].strip(), '->', count(sec.split('\n', 1)[1]))
EOF
```

It prints `333` for each of the two summary headings.

## Vision 2525 — the PDF paper, thirteen sections

Vision 2525 — Humanity's Coordination Framework is the thirteen-section PDF, and it argues one thing:
civilization has become too complex, too fast and too interconnected to coordinate through disconnected systems,
and the question is no longer whether advanced technology will shape society but whether humanity will shape it
with wisdom, visibility and discipline. Section I puts humanity at the centre as the starting point rather than
the afterthought. Human Intelligence defines purpose, ethics, acceptable risk and final authority; Collective
Intelligence turns experience and field signals into visible priorities; AI accelerates pattern recognition,
forecasting, translation and simulation. The first doctrine is stated plainly and never softened: humanity
decides, technology assists, trust must be proven.

Sections II through XII are the architecture, each a named layer resting on the one beneath it. R-CORE is the
civilization nervous system — recursive, continuous, operational. COMM-2525 is the signal fabric that holds when
pressure rises. LINK-2525 carries mission intent so purpose survives scale, not merely connectivity. EDGE-2525
puts intelligence at the point of need, because delay becomes danger. SYNC-2525 is memory: operational truth
preserved when events outrun the institutions recording them. UCRS-2525 gives every domain one spatial language.
Reality plus Simulation Learning rehearses decisions before they become irreversible. Collective Intelligence
acts; HI/AI/SI governance supplies conscience; SSSES — Security, Stability, Scalability, Efficiency,
Succinctness — proves readiness before capability expands; the Flywheel turns qualified ideas into measured
value that funds the next improvement.

Section XIII returns to the choice: drift through fragmentation, or build something worthy of the intelligence
being created. What holds the document together is a single disciplined ladder — pilot, replay, qualify,
certify, adopt, educate, expand — which any city, classroom, agency or emergency team can enter with one
coordination problem, scaling only what earns trust. Worth noting: that ladder is already running inside your
code as the seven Light Codex levels. The document's register is institutional and visual — thirteen diagrams,
commander takeaways, key outcomes — and its promise is measurable rather than rhetorical: faster coordination,
less waste, stronger resilience, clearer accountability, preserved dignity.

## Vision • 2525 online, nineteen sections

Vision • 2525 online is a different document about the same future, entered from the other end entirely. Where
the PDF describes architecture, the nineteen-section web version addresses you directly, in the second person,
about value and the rules that protect it. Its shape is deliberate and stated outright: the Seed of Life is
seven circles, Vision 2525 is thirteen, and the full Flower of Life is nineteen — one centre, six that touch it,
and twelve more that the six make possible. Section 1, The Unit, is that centre and everything else touches it:
the local hour as the anchor of worth, counted the same wherever in the world it happens to be worked.

The inner six are the mechanism and its brakes. The Ceiling and the Floor set what a single hour can reach and
what a good year protects in the years afterwards. The Wall separates what may be claimed from what may not. The
Shield defends the contributor rather than the framework itself. The Ledger records, append-only, so that
nothing is quietly edited after the fact. The Settlement pays, and publishes its own lateness together with the
cause. The Off Switch halts the machine by arithmetic rather than by conscience — and five of its six stopping
conditions turn against the framework, not against the people inside it. None require anyone's courage.

The outer twelve are what those six make possible: what we cannot see, the past, the present and the future, the
ladder, the proof before replacement — where a challenger must reproduce five thousand recorded answers exactly
and then beat the incumbent by a full ninth — the innovation pod, the funding loop, the legal pathway, the
contest, and all of the intelligences. Section 19 closes on the weakest clause in the whole structure, and says
so openly: a request enforced by nothing at all and refusable at zero cost. Three people, one shared problem,
one recorded hour. The document ends by handing its experiment to a reader who owes it nothing.
