The finding that decides everything

Grok's principle forbids something this project specified but never built. Adopting it costs nothing — and the window closes the moment it ships.

Grok's rule is absolute: no token-weighted, stake-weighted, or contribution-weighted voting on governance decisions. That contradicts Token_Governance_Math.md:76, which defines W_base = SI_balance + (HI_balance × 0.5) — governance weight rising with contribution — and :146-160, which multiplies it by a tenure-and-consistency reputation score reaching 6×.

None of it is implemented. W_base, HI_weight_factor, reputation_score, tenure_factor, consistency_bonus, damping_factor and effective_governance_weight appear in zero source files. What is implemented — quadratic weighting at cube7_ranking/ranking_aggregation.py:32-43 — governs theme prioritization, not who holds authority.

So the resolution is not a compromise. Sovereignty is unweighted; prioritization may be weighted. That distinction is already the state of the code. The only action required is to delete the specification that would have broken it — and to do so before anyone builds from it.

§ 1

Two votes, one wall

Almost every governance failure in decentralised systems comes from a single conflation: treating “what should we do?” and “who decides?” as the same ballot. They are not, and they should not share a weighting rule.

SovereigntyPrioritisation
DecidesWho leads · amendments · purpose · removalWhich themes rank · what gets built first
WeightingNone. One person, one voteQuadratic on contributed time, capped at 15%
Who may voteVerified natural persons onlySession participants
CorporationsZero — see §2May contribute signal; may not exceed the cap
StatusTo be built — nothing conflicting existsShipping today
Why this is defensible rather than a fudge

Weighting prioritisation by contributed time is epistemic: people who have done the work usually have better information about which work matters. Weighting sovereignty by anything is political: it makes some humans count more than others. The first is sensemaking; the second is a franchise. Conflating them is how one-person-one-vote quietly becomes one-dollar-one-vote.

The boundary must be written into Document 0 as a definition, not left to interpretation — because every future proposal to weight sovereignty will arrive framed as improving prioritisation.

§ 2

The constitutional article

Article · Human Primacy of Power

No investor, corporation, government, institution, fund, treasury, or coordinated group of entities shall ever hold greater governance, voting, or directional power within the System than any single natural person. All economic, technical, and organisational designs are subordinate to this principle.

One amendment to Grok's formulation

Grok proposes institutions receive “zero voting power, or at most the same single unit as any individual.” Take zero. The alternative is a category error with a predictable failure mode: a corporation is not a person, and granting it a person-equivalent unit concedes the exact fiction that produces capture. It also creates an arbitrage — an institution with one unit and ten thousand employees has an incentive to route influence through the employees, which is precisely the coordination Grok's own anti-aggregation rule then has to police.

Institutions contribute work, data, funding, facilities and contracts. They receive transparency obligations, published caps, and public acknowledgement. They do not receive a ballot. Nothing is lost: an institution's people already vote, as people.

The design test, made falsifiable

Apply before accepting any feature

“If a well-resourced corporation, government, or investor group tried to dominate this system, what concrete mechanism stops them from ending up with more power than one ordinary participant?”

Grok is right that an answer relying on culture is insufficient. Make it stricter still: the answer must name a file, a test, or a legal instrument. “Governance policy forbids it” is not an answer. “The mint function accepts a duration and has no currency parameter, asserted by test_mint_rejects_currency” is. Every safeguard in §4 is written to that standard, and the ones that cannot yet meet it are marked open.

§ 5

The unsolved problem: proof of personhood

Stated plainly, because this audience will ask first

One-person-one-vote requires knowing who is one person. This is not solved, here or anywhere. Every deployed approach trades one concentration for another, and Grok names the trap exactly: the Sybil defence must not itself become a mechanism only the wealthy or well-documented can pass.

ApproachConcentration it creates
Government IDExcludes the undocumented, stateless, and refugees; makes states gatekeepers of a system designed to be independent of them
BiometricsIrrevocable if breached; hardware and vendor dependence; strong resistance in several target jurisdictions
Social graph / web of trustAdvantages the already-connected; reproduces existing social hierarchy as franchise
Stake or depositWealth becomes the qualification — a direct violation of the article
In-person verificationEgalitarian and hard to fake; expensive and slow; does not scale to a billion
Recommended posture: federated, plural, and disclosed

No single global personhood authority — that authority would itself become the most concentrated power in the system, which the article forbids. Instead: each signatory region verifies persons under its own lawful method, publishes which method it used, and the system records the method on the franchise. Plurality prevents any one verifier from becoming a chokepoint; disclosure means every ballot's provenance is inspectable.

Two honest consequences. First, franchise quality will vary by region, and that must be visible rather than hidden — Disclosure of Absence applied to the electorate. Second, this caps credible scale until verification scales. A system that claims a billion voters it cannot verify has not solved Sybil; it has hidden it. Better to grow the franchise at the speed it can be verified and say so.

This is the weakest joint in the architecture, and it is stated as such deliberately. A framework claiming Human Primacy while waving at personhood would be claiming a property it cannot deliver.

§ 6

eXeL AI's split is adopted: QUAI for public coordination and audit anchors, QI for cash-like contributor settlement, and neither for governance. The rule that makes it safe is one line: external token holdings map to no internal authority. Someone holding a large QUAI position holds exactly the governance power of someone holding none.

Operational caution eXeL AI raised, and it is load-bearing

QUAI↔QI conversion is protocol-rate and subject to a lock period. Settlement promises must not assume instant liquidity. Any projected payout must disclose timing, conversion status, currency, tier, rate date, and unresolved risk — the same disclosure discipline the settlement ladder already requires. Promising same-day settlement across a two-week lock is the kind of small commitment that becomes a solvency problem at scale.

Equally: do not promise privacy where the transaction path is transparent. Native QI is the privacy-preserving rail; swap paths are not. State which one a given payout used.

The contribution receipt

One structured record per contribution, hashed on-chain and held in full off-chain: receipt_id · contributor · project · role · council sponsor · verified time · deliverable · replay ref · qualification · 웃 allocated · annual 웃 total · jurisdiction · settlement tier · rate + currency + as_of_date · multiple applied · settlement txn · unresolved fields. The last field is the one most systems omit: a receipt must be able to say what it does not know.

§ 7

Why education, innovation and real work are one system

This is the part that is easy to state as aspiration and hard to state as mechanism. The mechanism is the contribution receipt.

In most economies a student's work is simulated — graded, then discarded. Its value is a signal about the student, not an artifact anyone uses. That is why the education-to-work transition is expensive, slow, and unfair to anyone without networks. The three activities are separate because their outputs are separate: coursework produces grades, innovation produces IP, work produces wages.

The mechanism that fuses them

A contribution receipt does not care whether its author is a student, a professional, or a retiree in a village with a phone. It records verified time, a deliverable, a council sponsor, a replay reference, and a qualification outcome. The same artifact is simultaneously a transcript entry, a portfolio item, a governance-standing record, and a settlement claim.

So a fifteen-year-old who contributes eight qualified hours to a real de-risking project has, from one act: evidence for a credential, a public work record, standing in the system that produced it, and 웃 that settles at their local rate. Nothing was simulated. Nothing was discarded.

ConventionalUnder this system
Coursework → grade → discardedContribution → receipt → replayable artifact
Credential asserts capabilityQualification demonstrates it, with replay evidence
Unpaid internships gate access by family wealthContribution settles at local rate from the first qualified hour
Age is a proxy for competenceQualification is measured; age is not an input
Innovation happens after educationInnovation is the education, on real de-risking work

For crowdsourcing researchers there is a specific claim worth testing here. Open-innovation platforms reliably struggle with contributor retention and credit attribution. This design attacks both at once: attribution is the receipt, and retention is addressed because contribution accrues standing and settlement rather than only reputation points. Whether that materially improves retention is an empirical question this architecture makes measurable — receipts, replay and qualification produce exactly the longitudinal data such a study needs.

§ 8

The legal frame

Four properties, one per risk. Entity forms are patterns; the vehicle is jurisdiction-specific and belongs to counsel.

PropertyCloses
No equityNothing to acquire. An entity without shares cannot be bought or taken over.
Asset lockNothing to extract. Assets may transfer only to another purpose-locked body.
Control severed from capitalNothing to capture. Economic and governance rights are different instruments with no conversion.
Federated domestic personalityNothing to switch off. A domestic organisation per region — not one foreign entity soliciting inward.
The question for counsel, in one sentence

Not “which entity should we use?” but “in this jurisdiction, which form makes Human Primacy irreversible by a future board?” A principle a successor can amend away is a preference, not a protection — and the successors who will want to amend it do not exist yet and will be more persuasive than anyone arguing today.

On regulator exposure: with 웃 non-purchasable and no economic return to funders, the securities question largely closes in every target jurisdiction. What binds instead is charitable solicitation, AML/CFT, payments and stored value, pension and deferred compensation, data protection, and foreign-funding restriction — with India's FCRA, UAE's criminal fundraising-permit regime, and Russia's foreign-agent rules the three hard cases. The federated form in §8 is not only a resilience choice; in India it may be the only workable one.

§ 9

How the framework amends itself

The operator's requirement is that the polling engine evolve the framework over time. That is achievable and it is also where a subtle capture vector hides, so the mechanism has to be stated precisely.

StageMechanismVote type
1 · SurfaceAnyone raises an issue through the feedback loop, auto-tagged to cube and requirement—
2 · ClusterAI clustering into themes; determinism required — identical input, identical themes—
3 · RankQuadratic ranking, 15% capPrioritisation (weighted)
4 · DraftCouncils draft the amendment; conflicts with existing clauses computed, not asserted—
5 · SimulateReplay the amendment against historical decisions: what would have changed?—
6 · RatifyAdoption or rejectionSovereignty (1p1v, unweighted)
7 · RecordClause versioned, superseded text retained, dependency map regenerated—
The capture vector inside the amendment process itself

Stages 1–5 are agenda control, and agenda control is power. A well-resourced actor unable to buy votes at stage 6 can still flood stage 1, shape clustering at stage 2, and dominate what ever reaches a ballot. Every mature deliberative system has been attacked this way.

Three defences, all measurable: a per-person submission rate limit (volume must not buy salience); published clustering determinism, so theme formation can be re-derived and challenged rather than trusted; and a citizen-initiative path by which a threshold of individual members can force an item to ratification without council sponsorship — so the councils cannot become a silent veto.

Stage 5 is the piece most governance systems lack and this one can actually do, because replay already exists. An amendment can be tested against history before it is adopted: re-run past decisions under the proposed rule and show which outcomes change. That converts constitutional debate from prediction into evidence — and it is the single strongest reason to keep the ledger deterministic.

§ 12

Master of Thought — synthesis

Vision 2525's diagrams describe a civilisation that observes, learns, simulates, verifies, improves and evolves. Grok supplied the constraint that decides whether such a system stays human: no accumulation of capital or contribution may ever exceed one person's voice. eXeL AI supplied the discipline that makes it survivable: never let voting, membership, contribution, allocation, compensation, settlement, revenue and ownership become one instrument. They are the same insight approached from politics and from accounting.

The synthesis is one wall and one honest gap. The wall separates a franchise from an economy and is enforced in code paths and entity structure rather than in policy. The gap is personhood, which nobody has solved and which this document declines to pretend it has. Everything else — the seven instruments, the receipt, the settlement ladder, the amendment loop, the attack surface — is engineering around those two facts.

What makes this worth a serious reader's time is not ambition; it is that the central claim is falsifiable. Count the code paths by which capital can reach a governance vote. The target is zero.
