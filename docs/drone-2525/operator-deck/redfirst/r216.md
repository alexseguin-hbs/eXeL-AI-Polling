# Red first — Drone-2525 r.216 · a full magazine reads full

**His words (operator, 2026-10-19, with a screenshot whose corner reads eXeL r0.178):**

> heres one revision where 30 filled up to the top ofnthe magazine

**Invariant:** *A full magazine reads full — at the maximum round count the fill reaches the top of the
magazine body — and every round from one to the maximum draws a different picture.*

## The measurement that started it

Both images profiled the same way: the ink extent of the magazine drawing, then every raster row where lit
pixels cross ≥ 30 % of the body width.

| | fill starts | fill ends |
|---|---|---|
| **r0.178 — his picture, 30 RDS** | **6.7 % from the top** | 87.2 % |
| **r.215 — shipped, 30 RDS** | **39.2 % from the top** | 100 % |

r.215's figure is the served `play.html` rendered headless at 428×926 DPR 3 with
`state.mag={cap:30,rounds:30}` and `hudMag()` called.

## RED on the r.215 bytes

Two assertions added to `frontend/tests/magazine-rasters.test.mjs`, run before any source change:

```
FAIL: a FULL magazine reads full — the first bar starts within 10 % of the top (BARS.json 32%, deck MAG_FILL_TOP 32.0%)
FAIL: every round draws its own picture — 30 rounds in a full magazine, 19 distinct fills (a clamp that collapses the top rounds hides them)
magazine-rasters: 31 passed, 2 failed
```

**They are red on every revision from r.203 to r.215** — that span is the proof they were always needed.

### One of them was wrong the first time, and the error is kept here on purpose

As first written, the second assertion **passed** on r.215. It looped `r = 1 … tops.length`, i.e. to the
**bar count** (19), so it never reached the collapsed range at all. The collapse is at `r = MAG_BARS … cap`.
It now reads the capacity out of the deck's own `magCap()` and loops `r = 1 … 30`, which is what turns it
red with `19 distinct fills`. A gate that cannot reach the defect is not a gate, and this one could not.

## Also red-first, inside the deck

`ROUNDS_ARE_INSIDE_THE_MAGAZINE` (`play.html`, deferred boot QA) carried `&& top > 25` — r.203's
*"below the window"* rule written into the deck's own self-test, which **actively forbids this fix**. It
becomes `top <= 10`, and the row's note changes from *"below the window"* to *"full to the top"*.

## What is deliberately NOT asserted

Anything about the witness window. r.203 moved the bars below it for a real reason; his own r0.178 picture
covers it at a full magazine (the row profile runs unbroken through its position), so the window is covered
when full and uncovers as he fires. If he wants it clear again that is a new picture from him.
