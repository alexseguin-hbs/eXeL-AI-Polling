its velocity (level), else a numeric `d.yaw` flies level at that heading, else the nose-up hover — and `foilToDeck()` turns
every body point by roll, pitch and yaw (the deck's own yaw sense: forward = (−sin yaw, 0, cos yaw), as `units[].yaw`),
then lands it at the track's own place. The top point of his picture is the nose: the front. The row's rotation leg sets
`d.att = {yaw: cam.yaw − π/2, pitch: 0, roll: 0}`, draws, measures the nose right of the tail and the side-on ratio 3.1,
