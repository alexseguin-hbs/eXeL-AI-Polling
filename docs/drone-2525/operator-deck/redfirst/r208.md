## 4 · The range, played rather than asserted

The same scripts, against the r.207 bytes and the shipped r.208 bytes, with the wall-clock field dropped:

| play-through | r.207 | r.208 | identical |
|---|---|---|---|
| TRAIN UP (`scripts/drone-trainup-play.mjs`) | 34 entries | 34 entries | **yes, byte for byte** |
| QUAL 40 (`scripts/drone-qual40-play.mjs`) | 168 entries · 40 shots · 3 reloads · 40 of 40 EXPERT · 231 s | 168 entries · identical throughout | **yes, byte for byte** |

And after boot, with a player who had aimed at 900 ms: pan 35.5 / tilt 7.25 kept on both, the label unchanged on both,
one pop-up standing on both, `state.phase` `moving` on both.

**One thing about the range is deliberately different, and it is a defect of r.207 being closed.** Through r.207's own
challenge picker, CH1 then CH0 leaves the range at `phase:'static'`, and four hits then toast `PASS · MOVING TARGETS`
with `passed` 1 → 2. r.208 would have added a second door onto that, so `goRange()` now hands the range's wave back
whole: on these bytes both round trips leave `phase:'moving'` and the quota toasts the range's own TRAIN UP line.
`goRange` is re-pinned in the freeze under its own key with that reason.

**Three of my own leaks were caught before the push, none of them by the freeze.** The first cut left the range at
`phase:'static'` with its pop-ups down, so `waveCheck()` fired `PASS · MOVING TARGETS` at the player's fourth hit —
the QUAL 40 timelines diverged at entry 18 and nothing else showed it. The second cut handed the 42 turrets back
through `parkRangeTurrets()`, which zeroes the optic, so a player who had aimed lost the aim at 1.5 s. The third was the row written to catch the first: it restored the
state one line before it measured the drift, so it could not fail — two deliberate tampers left it green. It now
measures what the DOOR left, from a normalised range, and only then cleans up after itself; re-run against a deck
whose `goRange` no longer hands the wave back it goes red naming `phase,hits`, and against one that wipes all 42
aims it goes red naming the turrets. All three are closed.
