## What is deliberately NOT restored

His image 2 shows the link as `/drone-2525/play?range=21&role=turret&mode=train-up` — **without `.html`**. That
was the r.186 form; r.196 corrected it on purpose, and `.github/workflows/verify-live.yml` fetches

the live check. **Only the look is restored.** The patcher refuses on `/drone-2525/play?range=` appearing in
code.

## One trap the patcher had to learn, again

The stale check first refused on `/drone-2525/play?range=` — found at line 6129, inside the **embedded R-CORE
ledger** r.210 added, in a historical entry whose prose *describes* the old link. The patcher now strips the
`RCORE_LEDGER` block before any code check, exactly as `drone-playable.test.mjs` learned to do in r.210. **The
embedded record is data, not code**, and this is the second time that has bitten.

## The range is untouched, proven by PLAY on the exact bytes

Both play-throughs were run against the r.211 cut and the carried r.210, served side by side.

**TRAIN UP — IDENTICAL**, field for field with the clock dropped: the fifteen `looks`, the three hits, the
magazine counts, the double-tap leg, the sky-tap refusal and the 3.3× caption measurement.

**QUAL 40 — IDENTICAL in behaviour**, 40/40 EXPERT on both, 3 reloads, 0 errors, 231 s, the same closing strip
`CEASE FIRE · 40 OF 40 · EXPERT · RANGE IS COLD · PRESS RESTART`.

Said precisely rather than glossed, because the raw timelines are **not** byte-equal — 166 entries against 164:

- 36 vs 34 of the difference is the harness's own `phantom-check` sampling note, which varies run to run on
  unchanged bytes (proven at r.209: two runs of the same deck gave 167/37 then 168/38).
- Of the **130 paired entries** that remain, the **only two fields that EVER differ are `strip` and `light`, in
  3 entries** — and in every one of those three the difference is purely the **seconds number of the live
  countdown** (`11 S` vs `12 S`), i.e. the harness sampled one second apart. Zero entries differ by anything
  other than that number.
- With the countdown-bearing fields dropped, the two timelines are **IDENTICAL**.
