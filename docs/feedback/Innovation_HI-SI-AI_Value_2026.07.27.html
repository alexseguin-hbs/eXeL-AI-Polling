05Why it works — the SoI Trinity

The leverage is not "AI wrote code fast." It is three intelligences composed: judgment sets direction, a shared-intent council catches what one mind misses, and AI executes at the speed of thought.

웃
Human Intelligence
The operator: what to build, for whom, and what "good" means. ~13 h of direction, taste, and go/no-go judgment — the scarce input the whole system multiplies.
♡
Shared Intent
The 12 Ascended Masters council + MoT — a shared-intent review that stress-tests each change through 12 lenses (security, math, a11y, mobile, edge cases) before it ships.
◬
Artificial Intelligence
Execution: writing, testing, gating, and committing — 73 verified commits and 239 tests in three days, each tsc-clean and build-compiled.
