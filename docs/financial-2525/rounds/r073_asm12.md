## Christo: consensus and user flow (first-time sign-in, Accrual Units, budget, record, "saved to your account") · B- · FIX-FIRST

I walked the built page as a new person, signed in on a phone. All five gates pass: surface 221, accrual 151, CRS 597, sync probe 20, layout smoke 94. A first deposit lands in In Escrow, accrues at $1.3889 a minute, and the mark turns green about two seconds later. A second tab and a fresh device show the same entry and figures. Budget refusals are named, a good figure saves, and the other device picks it up. One path fails. If a phone edits the budget while the account can't be reached, and a laptop then opens the page for the first time, the laptop's untouched default budget wins. The phone's edit is gone and its mark still says saved.

**11 words:** Budget edit made offline is lost to another device's untouched default.

- **[blocker]** A device's untouched default budget (the sheet, never edited) goes up to the account dated now. Any device that edited the budget while the account was unreachable then takes that default over its own edit when it reconnects. The edit is lost silently and the cloud mark reads 'saved'. This contradicts FD-103 and the sync probe's claim that the budget never goes back to an older copy.
  - evidence: Reproduced on the built page (christo/race.mjs). Phone B with account reads failing: edit Rent to 2000, Net 1,474.10 becomes 174.10, nothing sent. Laptop A, first visit with reads working: writes the fin-plan row at=1790984134775 holding the sheet. B gets the online event: Net goes back to 1,474.10 and the cloud mark reads 'saved'. Still the same after 30 s. Cause: command-ux1.tsx:368 `at0 = ch === "send" && planAt > 0 ? planAt : nextStamp(...Date.now())`, so a never-edited plan (planAt 0, no remote row) is stamped now. Cards use needsTime and stay at:0, which is why fin-cards was written {cards:[],at:0} on the same first visit (walk7.mjs). syncChoice (cloud.ts:60) then makes the 'newer' default win.
  - fix: Treat a never-edited budget the way the cards are treated. Send it with at 0, or don't send it at all, when planAt is 0. A real edit (at > 0) must always beat an untouched default. Add this to fin-sync-probe: phone B edits offline, device A makes a first visit, B reconnects, and B's lines survive on both devices.
- **[question]** Every newly signed-in person starts with the operator's own budget figures (Income 3,924.49, Rent 700 and the rest) as their personal budget. The figures are written to their account on first load and the mark shows green, even though the person typed nothing.
  - evidence: walk7.mjs with sub auth0|brand-new-person: on first load the account holds a fin-plan row with the sheet's lines (A.income_wages 3604.49, B.rent_mortgage 700…). Signed-out visitors see these figures labelled 'EXAMPLE · the operator's paycheck as data'. Signed-in visitors see them unlabelled.
  - fix: Operator's choice: start new people from an empty or labelled-example budget, or keep the sheet. Either way, don't count the default as the person's saved budget (this ties into the blocker).
- **[nit]** For a first-time person, 'Record it' stays greyed out after an amount is typed, with nothing on screen saying a Type must be chosen first.
  - evidence: walk2.mjs: after filling the amount, the button is still disabled and the form shows no hint. Code: command-ux1.tsx:840 `disabled={!txType}`.
  - fix: Show a one-line hint under Type, or leave the button enabled and have it say 'Choose Deposit or Withdrawal' when pressed (lexicon key staged English-only).
