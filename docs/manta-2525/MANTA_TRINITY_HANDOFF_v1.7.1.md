Current conceptual chemistries:

* LFP for Mini pods
* LiFePO₄ / marine battery systems for Sail Ark
* high-capacity battery banks for Sentinel platforms

Required validation:

* thermal runaway analysis
* gas detection
* toxic gas handling
* fire suppression
* pressure relief
* battery compartment isolation
* passenger-space separation
* emergency power independence
* DNV Battery(Safety) / Battery(Power) or equivalent pathway
* class-society review

EV battery inside the pod must be treated as a separate hazard.

Tesla-class vehicle conceptual handling:

    Traction battery disconnected
    High-voltage isolation verified
    Thermal monitoring active
    Dedicated gas sensing
    Physical containment
    Emergency vent path
    No charging while submerged unless certified

## 16. Emergency Egress Doctrine

Design doctrine:

No reliance on ambient-pressure escape at operating depth.

Preferred hierarchy:

1. Vehicle ascends to surface, then occupants exit.
2. Mothership ascends to shallow rescue condition.
3. Rescue vessel / ROV assists recovery.
4. Certified escape capsule or rescue trunk, if separately approved.
5. Technical diving escape only as last resort, not design basis.

Status:

Design doctrine only.
Rescue and human-factors validation remain 🔴 Red.
