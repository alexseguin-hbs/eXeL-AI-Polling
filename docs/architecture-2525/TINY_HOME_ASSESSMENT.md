## Executive Summary
This 30′ × 30′ (900 ft²) tiny home design is a clean, disciplined 3×3 voxel layout — exactly aligned with the eXeL AI voxel philosophy. Each room is a precise 10′ × 10′ × 10′ voxel, creating a simple single-story rectangular footprint with a front porch and stairs.

The latest iteration shows meaningful improvements in master suite consolidation and overall zoning. The strict voxel grid remains highly suitable for parametric tools, prefab construction, accurate cost modeling, and future modular expansion.

**Overall Grade: B+** — Excellent structural and modular concept. Needs refinement for flow between Living/Kitchen/Dining and optimization of natural light and storage placement.

## Part IV — Functional Flow Assessment
**Current Flow (Improved but Still Suboptimal):** Entry / Porch → Storage/Laundry → Kitchen area (indirect). Master suite is now properly consolidated at the rear, which is a clear improvement.

**Recommended Revised Grid — Option A (Improved 3×3)**
```
+---+---+---+
| O | B | B |    O = Office (front, good natural light)
+---+---+---+    B = Bedroom (with egress window)
| L | K | D |    L = Living Room (open to Kitchen)
+---+---+---+    K = Kitchen (island, open to L/D)
| M | M | C |    D = Dining (adjacent to Kitchen)
+---+---+---+    M = Master Bedroom (rear, private)
                 C = Master Closet + Bath (consolidated rear)
```
Key improvements over the current image: middle row opened for flow/perceived space; master suite consolidated at rear with privacy; laundry/storage relocated near master suite; clear public vs. private zoning; island becomes the natural gathering point.

**Recommended Revised Grid — Option B (Open Concept):** Combine Living + Kitchen + Dining into one Great Room while retaining the 3×3 structural grid. This maximizes light, flexibility, and the "tiny home" spacious feel.

4. Enhance the front porch — make larger or partially wrap for better outdoor living.
