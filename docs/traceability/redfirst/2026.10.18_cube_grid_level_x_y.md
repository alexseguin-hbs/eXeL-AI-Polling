# Red first — the cube grid reads (Level, X, Y), his order

> **Operator, 2026.10.18:** *"ah thats right:  Level, X, Y"*, and earlier the same day:
> *"we spiral from 2,2,1 position for cube 1, go up to 2,3,1, then right to 3,3,1…"*

**DD-94.** The coordinates in this repo were never wrong. The renderer read the triple as `(row, col,
level)` with row 1 at the top, which draws a 90° rotation of his grid. The word that settles it is his
own: from (2,2) to (2,3) is only *up* if the second field is a Y that counts upward.

```
the triple read as (row, col, level), row 1 at top  ->  7 8 9 / 6 1 2 / 5 4 3   does NOT match his grid
the triple read as (level, x, y), y upward          ->  9 2 3 / 8 1 4 / 7 6 5   MATCHES exactly
```
