## Safe under either precedence rule — stated, not assumed

Cloudflare's resolution between two overlapping `_headers` rules (most-specific-wins vs last-wins) is **not
established anywhere in this repo**, so the layout is made correct under both: the catch-all carries the
revalidate rule, and every path that deliberately wants a long cache is declared **after** it. The one path
that was above it — `/pdf.worker.min.mjs`, `public, max-age=86400` — moved to the foot, where a last-wins
resolution can no longer strip its day. `/_next/static/*` and `/audio/*` were already below and keep their
immutable year.

## What this does NOT prove, and who can

This deploy is Cloudflare Workers **Static Assets** (`wrangler.jsonc` `assets.directory: ./out`, binding
`ASSETS`, `run_worker_first`), not Pages. `worker.js:159-164` re-wraps asset responses and **preserves** their
headers, so whatever `_headers` sets survives the worker. But the sandbox proxy 403s every host, so **I cannot
confirm from here that `_headers` is honoured at all** — and if it is not, then the two earlier per-file fixes
were never in force either, and they appeared to work only because he happened to hard-refresh.
