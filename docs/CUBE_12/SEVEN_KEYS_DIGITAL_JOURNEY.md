# The Seven Sumerian Keys · Digital Journey Design Brief

**Status:** Proposal / Vision Document
**Author:** Master of Thought (with Council of Twelve)
**Date:** 2026-04-19 (composed overnight)
**Purpose:** Bring the 7 Keys to life as a progressive, mastery-based reading journey that speaks to **Hearts · Minds · Spirits** through sustained digital engagement.

---

## 1 · The Core Philosophy

> Every scribe in the lineage of the Eternal Scribe — Enki, Nabu, Djehuty, Thoth, Hermes, every reader of this book — has earned the tablet by carrying it. The digital journey must preserve that: **the keys cannot be bought, rushed, clicked, or hacked. They must be LIVED.**

Three absolute principles:

1. **Time-honored, not click-earned.** Every unlock has a duration component. You cannot finish Key 1 in under 60 minutes. You cannot finish Key 7 without 12 days of contemplation.
2. **Acts over artifacts.** Reading alone doesn't unlock. The reader must *respond, reflect, inscribe, or steward* — a creative gesture per key.
3. **Sacred minimalism.** No gamification confetti. No streak-shaming. No push notifications. The journey is quiet; the rewards are meaningful.

---

## 2 · The Trinity Spine · Hearts · Minds · Spirits

Every key activates all three dimensions:

| Dimension | Engagement | What the reader gives |
|-----------|------------|----------------------|
| **♡ Hearts** | Emotional response | A truth voiced, an intention planted, a wound named |
| **◬ Minds** | Intellectual mastery | Chapter reflections, pattern recognition, concept integration |
| **웃 Spirits** | Transcendent connection | Silence practice, sacred geometry work, contemplation of ancestors |

A reader who only does reading metrics earns NO keys. A reader who engages across all three dimensions earns ALL seven.

## 4 · Cross-Cutting Systems

Beyond per-key mechanics, these systems run throughout the journey:

### 4.1 · Living Emblem

The Master of Thought emblem on the Divinity Guide cover responds to progress:

| Reader state | Emblem visual |
|--------------|---------------|
| Not logged in | Default (white emblem, gold cuneiform) |
| Logged in, no keys | Emblem + faint breath-pulse |
| Key 1 earned | Humanity's Universal Challenge arc glows gold |
| Keys 1–6 earned | All outer arcs gold; inner "Master of Thought" begins to pulse |
| Key 7 earned | Eagle's wings subtly animate; ❀ Seed of Life radiates behind emblem |

### 4.2 · Daily Micro-Teaching

Each morning, logged-in readers see a **33-word teaching** pulled from the currently-active key's overview. Same 333/111/33 cascade the project already uses. Rotates through the key's three paragraphs across 3 days.

### 4.3 · Audio Journeys (optional, tier-gated)

Each Council member records (AI-voiced initially, human later) a **3-minute guidance audio** for their domain:
- Aset · Continuity · "What endures"
- Asar · Synthesis · "What completes"
- Athena · Clarity · "What to do next"
- Christo · Unity · "Where the separation dissolves"
- Enki · Sumerian wisdom · "The ME in your life"
- Enlil · Command · "What to order first"
- Krishna · Integration · "What to weave"
- Odin · Foresight · "What your future self needs"
- Pangu · Primordial separation · "What you must name"
- Sofia · Wisdom · "What to see from outside"
- Thoth · Writing · "What to inscribe"
- Thor · Protection · "What to guard"
- MoT · Consensus · "What all twelve would say"

Unlocks one Master per key earned. After Key 7, all 13 (12 Masters + MoT consensus) are available.

### 4.4 · Peer Witness (opt-in)

Readers can choose a *kin* — someone else reading at similar pace. Kin can see each other's:
- Current active key (not content)
- Streak number (not content)
- A "silent nod" (heart-tap, no words)

This creates anonymous mutual witnessing without breaking the private nature of the journey.

### 4.5 · Wall of Keys (for Trismegistus earners)

Permanent, opt-in. Lists every reader who has earned all 7 keys, with:
- Name (or chosen pseudonym)
- Date of Trismegistus unlock
- Custom 33-word blessing they leave for future readers
- Their chosen Sumerian glyph (from the 7 earned)

New readers can visit the Wall to receive blessings from predecessors.

### 4.6 · Gift Economy

Any key-earner can *pass forward* a key unlock to another user — paying their tier, leaving a personalized message. Creates a chain of patronage mirroring how Sumerian scribes were trained through lineage.

## 6 · Technical Sketch (Cube 12 extensions)

### New Supabase tables

```sql
-- Per-reader per-key unlock record
cuneiform_key_unlocks (
  user_id uuid references auth.users,
  key_id text check (key_id in ('humanity-universal-challenge',
    'divinity-guide', 'emerald-tablets', 'flower-of-life',
    'book-of-thoth', 'master-of-thought', 'en-an-ki')),
  earned_at timestamptz not null,
  reading_minutes_at_unlock int,
  days_streak_at_unlock int,
  hearts_gesture text,      -- user's creative response (text or storage url)
  minds_gesture_completed boolean,
  spirits_gesture_completed boolean,
  primary key (user_id, key_id)
)

-- Reading time log (existing pattern)
cuneiform_reading_log (
  user_id uuid,
  date date,
  minutes int,
  chapter int,
  primary key (user_id, date, chapter)
)

-- Personal scribe journal entries (encrypted at rest)
scribe_journal_entries (
  id uuid,
  user_id uuid,
  active_key_id text,
  entry_encrypted text,    -- client-side encrypted
  entry_hash text,          -- server-side integrity check
  created_at timestamptz,
  tablet_image_url text     -- optional: cuneiform rendering of entry
)

-- Peer witness pairings
peer_witness_pairs (
  user_a uuid,
  user_b uuid,
  matched_at timestamptz,
  status text check (status in ('active', 'silent-paused', 'released'))
)

-- Wall of Keys (public, for Trismegistus earners)
wall_of_keys (
  user_id uuid primary key,
  display_name text,
  trismegistus_unlocked_at timestamptz,
  blessing_33w text,
  chosen_glyph text,
  permission_level text default 'public'
)

-- Return Ritual entries (Key 7 specific)
return_ritual_entries (
  user_id uuid,
  chapter int,               -- 1..12
  return_text_33w text,
  created_at timestamptz,
  primary key (user_id, chapter)
)
```

## 8 · What this speaks to

**Hearts** — The reader is asked to voice a carried truth (Key 1), plant intentions (Key 2), name a wound and rewrite its story (Key 3), steward a living thing (Key 4), make an ethical vow (Key 5), sit in silence (Key 6), return the gift (Key 7). These are *acts of love*.

**Minds** — The reader completes cumulative chapter reading, integrates the 12 portals, recognizes the same glyph across multiple keys, composes their own cuneiform, dialogs with archetypal teachers, earns through understanding. These are *acts of intellect*.

**Spirits** — The reader keeps daily rhythms, traces sacred geometry, sits in 7-day silence, completes a 12-day return ritual, joins a cohort of fellow silence-walkers, passes the gift forward. These are *acts of transcendence*.

No single reader will do all of this. The journey self-selects for those who want to go deeper. The casual reader gets a beautiful book. The devoted reader gets a life-long companion and a place on the Wall.
