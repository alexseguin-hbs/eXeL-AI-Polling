## Thesis
Vision-2525 cube homes are **offshore modules with better finishes**. GIS's 75-year EPC spine — offsite yard
fabrication, MODS materials management, distributed staging yards, GIS siting, integrated renewables, and 24/7
robotic profiling — is the physical substrate under the Level-3 cubes. EcoSpheres validates it: prefab beats
onsite, **permitting is the #1 schedule killer**, and stage-gates + a Design Traceability Matrix keep every
module deterministic and auditable.

## Operating model
1. **Regional MODS-tracked staging yards** near housing markets (compress last-mile delivery of finished cubes).
2. **The shipped stacked room designer is the single cut-list source of truth** — its 2D/3D panes + per-room CAD
   dimensions (S5 `lib/dim-annot.ts`) emit robot G-code + an **RFID work-pack BOM** per cube. No re-drawing.
3. **A SHA-256 spec hash gates every cut job** — kills designer→cut-list drift (the replay/determinism ethos made
   physical: identical spec in → identical module out → zero onsite rework).
4. **Fabricate 90%+ offsite** (frame, MEP, per-room assets, renewables as spec inputs), certify-weigh, RFID-tag,
   sequence by install order, JIT-haul to a GIS-sited pad. **Site collapses to bolt-together hook-up.**
5. **eXeL polling governs every human fork** — layout, finishes, siting, load-out; Cube 25 quote-lock needs ≥1
   human signer; actuals back-propagate into Cube 24's world model.

## 5D Robotic Profiler (Project P005-V)
Lights-out 24/7 5-axis cutter/profiler; TAM ~$46B, ~15.9% CAGR. Absorbs polling-driven demand surges without
scarce welders. Deterministic tolerances → cubes mate with zero onsite rework. Digital-twin simulation de-risks
fit-up before metal is cut; inline QC + predictive maintenance protect the line.

## Consensus risk & metric
- **Risk:** oversize-load transport permits stall delivery → **pre-clear haul routes at siting, not load-out.**
- **Metric:** **yard-completion % per cube (target ≥90%)** gating load-out, tracked against **onsite-rework hours
  (<2)** in Cube 27 Delivery & Actuals.
