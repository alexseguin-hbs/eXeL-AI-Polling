## 2. Read as

When a phase layer is shown (his 20:04 word: a phase shows only its layer), the layer carries its MATERIAL TOTALS, read from the same libs that draw the runs (lib/mep-runs.ts · lib/architect-bom.ts — one source, never a second number):
- **Plumbing** — total supply piping (ft), total DWV/sewer piping (ft), the fixtures served (count), the water heater/service entry; per room and for the whole house (the nine rooms summed);
- **Electrical** — total wire (ft), outlets (count), circuits, service amps, the panel; per room and whole house;
- **HVAC (venting)** — total duct (ft), registers/vents (count), the air handler; per room and whole house.
The totals appear with the layer (on the room designer when a room is entered; on the house when not) and feed the BOM and the estimate — consistency law: data tables · analytics · Supabase updated together.
