## What this means for the pod, and what it must not do

- **The mint stays currency-free.** 웃 = M × T. The region changes nothing about what is minted; it decides
  only what a 웃 settles as. Any rate reaching the mint is the defect the paper spent thirty-four releases on.
- **Settlement is `$ = 웃 × the region's hourly rate`, in that region's own currency.** No conversion between
  currencies, ever — the paper does not publish exchange rates and I will not invent any.
- **A NULL rate is not zero and not a guess.** `unit.settle`: "No contributor settles at zero merely because
  their government has not yet legislated." Tiers 1-pending, 2 and 3 must each say what is actually missing
  and what would resolve it, and show no figure at all.
- **The rate is stamped at mint, never looked up later** (D9, the vintage rule) — the stamp already carries
  `rate` and `currency`; the pod has been passing null.
