# Drone-2525 QUAL · 40 — exposure times must be accurate (2026.10.04_03.56..35 CST)

> Operator, verbatim (2026.10.04): "make sure times for multiple targets on qual40 are accurate"

## Findings before the fix (served deck r.153)
- **Program values:** 1 up = 5 s · 2 up = 8 s · 3 up = 12 s · 4 up = 16 s (operator's program of record, 2026-09-23); 3 s between
  engagements; 9 s between phases.
- **Sources reachable from the sandbox** (search results; the pages themselves are blocked):
  - 3 s between engagements: confirmed.
  - 10 s between phases ("a ten second transition delay to allow the shooter time to reload and assume the next position"): confirmed.
  - The per-count exposure seconds are not stated in any reachable source, so 5/8/12/16 stays the operator's value.
- **Measured at normal frame rate (60 fps, lane 21):** engagement 1 = 5.02 s, engagement 4 (3 up) = 12.02 s, engagement 5 (4 up) =
  16.02 s, gaps 3.02 s, phase gap 9.02 s. Accurate.
- **Measured with slow frames (≈160 ms apart):** a 5 s exposure lasted **16.58 s** and a 3 s gap lasted **10 s**. The main loop caps each
  frame at 50 ms (`dt=Math.min(.05,…)`), and `rangeTick` runs inside `phys(dt)`, so below 20 fps the qualification clock runs slower than real
  time.

## Fix (drone r.154)
- The range clock counts wall time, capped at 0.25 s per frame so a backgrounded tab cannot skip an engagement. Physics keeps the 50 ms cap.
- The phase gap becomes 10 s (sourced).
- Gates: a deck QA row for the range clock, and a slow-frame timing check in `drone-range-all-lanes`.
