## Reading — DD-96, and the last sentence is the one that governs the copy

  1. **Login enabled** on `/divinity-guide`.
  2. **Signed in → the reading spot is remembered**: the **section**, the **page** and the **number** the
     reader left off at, saved when they leave mid-read and restored when they come back.
  3. **Signed in → the donation ask appears at most once every three chapters.**
  4. **Signed out → the ask is more frequent**, and carries an offer to sign in.
