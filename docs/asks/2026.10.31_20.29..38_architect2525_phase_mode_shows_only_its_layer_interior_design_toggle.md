## 2. Read as

1. **A phase mode is a LAYER view.** With a PHASE chip selected (Foundation · Framing · Electrical · Plumbing · …), the room designer draws ONLY that phase's key elements — Foundation: the slab outline, footings, anchor bolts, the datum; Framing: the studs, plates, headers over the openings (the openings themselves, as the framer sees them); Electrical: the panel, the wiring runs, the outlets on the walls; Plumbing: the water + sewer runs and the fixtures they serve; and so on — on the bare 10 × 10 ft room, with the furniture OFF.
2. **The furniture is a phase of its own: "Interior Design", one element in the strip, a toggle.** The bed, nightstands, desk, mirror (the Layout A′ pieces) draw when Interior Design is the selected phase (or toggled on); any other phase shows its own layer alone. Openings (door · window) belong to the shell/framing layers, not to the furniture.
3. Nothing is removed: every phase keeps its SPEC card; the designer keeps its palette and tools in the Interior Design phase (placing furniture is interior design); the Design-Settings ⚡/💧 toggles stay as the per-system switches inside the Electrical/Plumbing layers.
