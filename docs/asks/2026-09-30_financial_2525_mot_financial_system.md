## Reading of record (what the ask binds)

1. **A new 2525 Domain Play, "Financial-2525"** — a session/surface with its own login per user, that records real
   financial transactions (deposits first) as they happen, and shows financial management by **quarter = 91 days**
   (4 × 91 = 364) with **day 365 a DOWN day**.
2. **The year resets at perihelion**, everywhere, on Austin, Texas time in **CST standard (UTC−6, no daylight
   shift)** — day 1 of every Financial-2525 year begins at the perihelion instant.
3. **$/min is the unit of an individual's financials**: a logged deposit carries an amount, a deposit date and a
   **length** (the span the money covers), and accrues in real time at amount ÷ length-in-minutes. The MoT
   financial notes are the documented, plain-language record each figure derives from.
4. **The ◬ ♡ 웃 Session is the basis** for the R-CORE wireframe (the SoI POD session's clock-and-witness model —
   value accrued over measured time — applied to money), and **revision control is on from the first line**,
   using the Drone-2525 framework verbatim: Version 00.00 · revision 0.001, a domain JSON as the one source, a
   rendered CRS (`FIN-##.##`), an append-only traceability ledger, the R-CORE badge + compare panel, gates in
   `test:ci`.
5. Worked example: **$3,604.49 monthly**, posted 2026-09-29.
6. **Two tenants, one model (addendum):** Financial-2525 is **tested on an individual first** (the operator's own
   deposits) and then **used to establish financial tracking for a business** — the same record, calendar and $/min
   law serve a person and a company; a business is a login whose deposits and outflows are logged the same way.
