## Asar’s Five Lines (Scoring Gate)

Two of the five lines cannot honestly be scored on the lawn today:

- **“Defended assets preserved”** — The lawn has **no defended points**. The paper names the set (command post, fuel farm, ammunition transfer point, maintenance cluster); the lawn needs its own.
- **“Joint network stable”** — Has no meaning in a deck that fetches nothing. Needs definition or a stand-in.

**Collision blocking the rung:** The Capitol’s own four façades (`D-CAP-S/N/E/W`) are listed as pop-up targets. The record states: *“Every building door is a pop-up target.”* **The defended asset is currently shootable.** A rung whose pass condition is *“the asset is still there when it ends”* cannot exist until that is fixed.
