2. **The bedroom has its door (Layout A′ door-1, S wall, 3'-0" × 6'-8", from the Living Room) — what no surface draws is the convention.** Today: a filled green bar 0.3 ft inside the wall (room designer 2D), a solid slab (3D), a gold bar for M and a wrong 60° bow for every other room (house plan), nothing (house voxel), two rects on the Entry only (Build 4D), a half-opacity rect (Simulate environments). The convention: the wall is cut for the door's width between two jambs on the wall line; the leaf is a thin line from the hinge jamb, perpendicular, one width long (open at 90°); a thin quarter-circle arc centred on the hinge, radius the width, from the leaf's free end to the strike jamb; a bedroom door swings into the bedroom; the hinge on the jamb nearer the nearest corner so the open leaf lies against the adjacent wall. One helper draws it on every surface; Rotate flips the hinge.
