## The operator's words (verbatim)
> ensure global time in SI heart is recorded for educational and group projects so we can covert later for paid projects eventually to HI

## 1. Reuse what the app already has (no second clock)
- `frontend/lib/pod-clock.ts`: start/stop events (`ClockEvent` with who and when), `measure()` turns them into witnessed time,
  `witnessedMinutes()` — and its own note: *"♡ is one minute given (unit.heart)"*. `hhmmss()` prints it.
- The same law already reads: ♡ is recognition — it cannot be saved up, spent, traded or leveraged (decision D12, `si.clockless-ladder`);
  웃 (HI) is paid as M × hours. So what is kept now is the **witnessed time record**; ♡ is shown from it today, and a paid project can
  later value the **same witnessed hours** as 웃 under its own rules. The ♡ itself is never converted or sold.

## 2. What to record
1. **Per member, per project, per task:** start and stop events for every working stretch — capture, Level 1 annotation, Level 2 review,
   (later) training upkeep. Each event: member, project, task, device, time stamp.
2. **Global time:** every time stamp is stored in UTC (one world clock) and shown in the person's own time and in the app's form
   `YYYY.MM.DD_HH.MM..SS` (CST for the record files). Nobody's time changes meaning when they travel or a team spans countries.
3. **Witnessed, not typed:** time counts only while the app is open on that task and the person is active (a pause after a few
   minutes with no touch closes the stretch). Typed claims are allowed as notes but are never counted as witnessed.
4. **Tied to the work:** each stretch links to what it produced — pictures added, boxes drawn, boxes reviewed — so a minute is never
   counted without a trace of work.
5. **One record, append-only:** the stretches live in the project record beside the other actions; corrections are new lines,
   never edits.
6. **Offline:** stretches are kept on the device and sent when the connection returns; the device clock is checked against the world
   clock when online and the difference is recorded, not silently fixed.

## 3. What people see
- On the project: *"Team time: 41 h 12 min ♡"* and per member *"Your time: 3 h 05 min ♡"*, split by capture · Level 1 · Level 2.
- In each model version's credits: members, their witnessed time and their counts.
- In educational projects: a class view for the teacher (time and work per student), never a race.

## 4. Later: a project becomes paid (the operator's decision)
1. The owner marks the project paid from a date. From that date, witnessed hours can be valued as 웃 (HI) at the project's rate
   (M × hours), through the app's existing HI rules.
2. **Earlier educational / group time stays ♡** unless the operator decides a back-dated rule; the record makes either choice possible
   because every stretch is already kept with who, what, when and the work it produced.
3. Payment itself lives in the existing token/payment parts of the app, not in Sensor Fusion.
