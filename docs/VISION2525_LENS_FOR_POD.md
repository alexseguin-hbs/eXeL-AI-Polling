# Vision 2525 — the whitepaper in brief, and the constraints it places on the POD session

Source: `docs/i18n/exec-summary.en.json`, the frozen SHA-locked master, 71 blocks, 4,895 words. Read in full 2026-09-10.
This file is the **design lens**: no POD decision may contradict it.

## Part 1 — What the whitepaper says

**It begins with a person, not a technology.** Thought becomes intention, intention becomes action, action enters time — the
one resource no person recovers. The discipline joining awareness, intention, time, action, consequence and learning is
**Thought Mastery**. *Master of Thought is a responsibility, not a rank; an invitation within Humanity, not a throne above it.*

**Time is the first common measure.** A minute in Austin and a minute in Phnom Penh are the same duration even where labour is
valued differently. Three layers are kept deliberately apart so none can impersonate another:

| Layer | Answers | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Measurement of Time** | what actually passed | the clock is never inflated |
| **HI** | what qualified contribution emerged | `HI earned = M × hours`, M explicit and visible |
| **Settlement** | lawful economic value | separate again, local |

One hundred actual hours remain one hundred hours even when they produce more than one hundred HI. The 9,999 annual HI
boundary measures *recognised contribution*, not hours worked.

**Humanity decides. Technology assists. Trust must be proven.** Capability and authority are different questions: a system may
know more and calculate faster without acquiring the right to decide what Humanity should value. AI, Collective, Spiritual and
Human intelligence each contribute what the others cannot; the task is integration without confusion.

**Polling is a listening instrument, not a truth machine.** A majority cannot vote an unsafe bridge safe. A leader may
disagree with a poll but must preserve the disagreement and the reason. Minority warnings stay visible because today's
minority can be tomorrow's discovered truth.

**Simulation lets thought meet consequence before Humanity must** — and simulation is not reality. State assumptions, record
predictions, compare prediction with reality; the difference is learning rather than embarrassment.

**Replay is memory that can outlive us.** It preserves successive states rather than quietly replacing yesterday with today.
Compare shows what changed, Cause records why, Qualification states what the evidence permitted at that moment. What we owe
the future is not obedience to our conclusions but access to our reasoning: what did you know, decide, why, what happened,
what did you learn.

**R-CORE** is the recursion: signals → context → evidence → simulation and decision → outcomes → Replay → qualified learning
returning into the next action. Its durable elements are Intent, Identity, Provenance, Requirements, Risk, Evidence,
Authority, Qualification, Replay, Learning, Communications, Simulation, and Human judgment.

**The Humanity Innovation Engine** runs need → intent → listen → interpret → user stories → requirements, risk, estimates →
design → simulate → human experience → verify and validate → qualify → reality → Replay → learn. AI can accelerate every
stage **without owning the process**. The 360 → 33 → 11 compression is *a hypothesis, not a promise*, and **must be allowed to
fail honestly**: if evidence rejects it, preserve the result rather than protect the number. The real measure is less
avoidable rework, clearer intent, earlier risk discovery, better estimates and fewer repeated failures.

**Proof before scale.** Imagined, designed, simulated, verified, validated, qualified and ready are *different evidence
states*. When they collapse into one another, confidence travels farther than proof.

**Power is structured before personalities are known.** Capital does not purchase sovereignty; leadership carries an end date;
an Off Switch exists because no system should become immortal because its founders forgot to design an ending.

**Education transfers the journey, not only the answer** — Replay lets a student examine why alternatives failed.

**It must be provable in one hour.** "A five-century vision should be able to begin in one hour, otherwise its first step is
already too large." Three people. State the intent before starting. Work. Use AI if it helps. Disagree if necessary. Record
the outcome. Preserve what changed. Ask what was learned.

Closing sequence: Humanity imagines · Thought gives direction · Time records the contribution · AI expands possibility ·
Simulation prepares · Engineering proves · Humanity decides · Replay remembers · Education transfers · The next generation
begins ahead.

## Part 2 — What this makes binding for the POD session

**The POD session IS "One Recorded Hour" (k53–k54).** It is not a feature that happens to involve time; it is the whitepaper's
own smallest meaningful act, the thing that must "preserve its largest principles". Every requirement below comes from the
text, not from preference.

1. **Intent is stated BEFORE the clock starts, and is frozen.** k53: "State the intent before starting." A pod that lets you
   write the intent afterwards cannot Replay honestly.
2. **The clock and the recognition are two numbers, never one.** k18, k21: actual elapsed time is preserved unaltered; HI is
   `M × hours` with M shown. The UI must never display a single blended figure, and must never let recognition edit the clock.
3. **The AI accelerator is a hypothesis that can fail.** k42–k43: record the baseline target, record what actually happened,
   and **preserve the case where AI made it slower**. A pod that can only show improvement is protecting a number.
4. **Human judgment and AI assistance are distinguishable in the record.** k54 asks it directly: "Did the record distinguish
   Human judgment from AI assistance?" Every AI contribution is labelled as such, permanently.
5. **Disagreement survives.** k30, k54: the minority signal and the reason for deciding against it stay in the record.
6. **Resume is Replay-shaped: append, never overwrite.** k36: "preserves successive states rather than quietly replacing
   yesterday with today." Coming back to a pod must reopen its history, not a mutable blob. This matches the standing
   CLAUDE.md law: a new edition is an append, never an edit.
7. **A simulated completion is labelled simulation.** k33: "simulation is not reality." An AsM-simulated finish records a
   *prediction*; when the real outcome arrives, Compare shows the difference and that difference is the learning.
8. **Evidence states never collapse.** k44: a pod may say estimated, simulated, or actual — never let one word carry another's
   authority.
9. **Three people, one hour, no account.** k53: the entry cost must stay that low.
10. **The Replay questions are the acceptance test.** k54, verbatim: could someone who was not present understand what was
    attempted? Was actual time preserved honestly? Did an insight survive that would otherwise have disappeared? Did
    disagreement remain visible? Did the group discover an assumption? Would the next group begin better informed?

**The invariant this yields, in the user's terms:**
> *Time already recorded is never lost or altered, and the pod always reopens to exactly what happened — including what was
> only predicted, what was disagreed with, and what the AI did.*

That is the pod's equivalent of "a completed signature is never discarded", and like it, it will be enforced by a gate in the
build rather than by memory.

# Part 3 — The White Paper's own POD section, read at the source (added after the full 77,777-word read)

The Executive Summary is the 4,895-word door. The White Paper view is 77,777 words across 139 blocks, one of three enforced
sacred totals (Brief 3,333 · NOSE 9,999 · Paper 77,777, gated by `scripts/lv-gate-vision2525.mjs`). **§14 is the Innovation

## THE WITNESS RULE (`unit.witness`, verbatim)
> "웃 is minted only for **time clocked by the platform**, worked in a pod of three or more, documented by that pod, audited by
> that pod, and reviewed by people who gain nothing from the claim."

Eight steps, six performed by the pod: start/clock-in → work → stop → outcome documented → self-audit → evidence attached
(session link, unlisted recording, or a summary of at least sixty seconds) → cross review by one to three other pods or the
tri-council → appended to the ledger. Three rather than two, because *"Two people can quietly agree on a lie in private; three
must openly conspire."* Change class: **evidence must exist = Immutable; which tool carries it = Adaptive** — so store the
**hash** of the evidence. Dispute rate and overturned-claim rate publish monthly.

**THE VIOLATION IN TODAY'S POD.** `app/soi-session/page.tsx` has no clock at all — `grep setInterval|elapsed` returns zero —
and mints 웃 from a **hand-typed** `hours` string (`lib/pod-roster.ts:34`, input at `page.tsx:896`). The rule says 웃 is minted
*only* for time clocked by the platform. The pod currently mints for time **claimed by a person**. That is the single largest
doctrinal gap in the feature, and it is what "document time" asks us to close.

## THE ACCELERATOR MATHS (`unit.accel`)
A project carries a **fixed estimate** plus a set bonus per unit of time delivered early; finishing early **adds to** pay
rather than shrinking it. The estimate is **set and signed before work begins by a party with no stake in the payout**, and is
locked carrying id, version, scope, estimated cost and duration, quality and risk thresholds, the signing authority, and a
**Replay hash**. The outcome is witnessed by an **independent** party. The bonus is payable **only if ALL six hold**: schedule
improved · scope preserved · quality maintained or improved · risk did not materially worsen · SSSES qualification passed ·
Human Authority accepted the outcome. *"Faster is not automatically better; cheaper is not automatically better; and more AI is
certainly not automatically better."*

**THE GAP.** Today the baseline is typed at AUDIT time (`page.tsx:126`, panel at `:923`), after the work, by anyone, with no
signature, no lock, no hash, and none of the six conditions. `unit.aitoken` adds that ◬ is read "from the witnessed
acceleration **against a locked estimate** — not minted at a per-minute rate."

## The mint coefficient — settled by the financial section (2026-09-10)

The operator declined to rule on the settlement figure I flagged and said: *"read the financial section of Vision•2525
and this will be clear to you Master of Thought."* It is clear, and the answer was already in the document.

**The locked identity.** `unit.mintsettle` — `웃 = hours × (9,999 ÷ 2,080)` at mint, currency-free; `$ = 웃 ÷ 4.807 ×
stamped rate` at settlement. "Everyone arrives at 9,999 웃 on the same 2,080 hours." Defect 15 was opened and closed in
one release for a coefficient that broke this, and its own remedy is the rule I have now followed: *"The coefficient is
now derived (9,999 ÷ 2,080 = 4.807), so the identity cannot drift, and a test asserts it rather than a comment claiming
it."*

**The independent cross-check.** `fund.return` prices the Seed — 1/7 of an hour — at **0.6867 웃**, "at 4.807 per hour".
The Seed is $1.036 in Texas, so one 웃 is $1.5087, which is $7.25 ÷ 4.807. Two routes, four matching figures.

**What the pod had wrong.** `lib/pod-yug.ts` minted one 웃 per hour at base, putting the ceiling 9,999 hours away at 1×
— 4.8 full-time years to fill one year's payout. Corrected: `mint(hours, m) = hours × YUG_PER_HOUR × m` with
`YUG_PER_HOUR = YUG_CEILING / FTE_HOURS`, derived. One full-time year at 1× now lands exactly on 9,999.

## SUPERSEDES THE SECTION ABOVE — 웃 = M × T (operator ruling, 2026-09-10)

**The entry immediately above is wrong and is left in place only as the record of the error.** It asserts a
4.807 웃/hour mint coefficient. There is no mint coefficient. The operator ruled:

> Ensure the same nomenclature for global payment system is used
> HI token = 웃 = M*T
> where multiple is multiple of local min wage

**How I got it wrong, so the next session does not.** I read `unit.mintsettle` — `웃 = hours × (9,999 ÷
2,080)` — and treated it as the general mint. **The tell was in the formula itself: it has no M in it.** A
mint with no multiple cannot be the general mint; it is the mint at the one band where a full-time year lands
on 9,999, which is 4.807×. **4.807 is a multiple, never a coefficient** — `unit.multiples` and `paper.s1`
call it exactly that: "the reference multiple … arrived at by division rather than by choice."

**The weight of the text, swept block by block.** `웃 = M × hours` is the current text of ~10 blocks,
including **`front.locked`** (Immutable, Document 0: "웃 is denominated in multiples of local or regional
minimum wage … It is a multiple of a local hour") and **`exec.s1` at r280**, the document's highest release.
`unit.carry` states the consequence outright: "웃 = hours × multiple, **so the multiple is simply the
웃-per-hour rate**." It is the only formula from which `unit.multiples` (every row 9,999 ÷ M), `unit.carry`
(every row 2,080 × M) and all five worked human examples derive. `웃 = hours × 4.807` is one block.

**The repository's own gates already said so** — `scripts/exec-summary-verify-all.mjs:13` requires the
verbatim `HI earned = M × hours` in 33 languages; `qis-semantic-check.mjs:62` and
`replay-audit-vision2525.mjs:118` hard-assert it in `unit.ceiling`.

**`unit.mintsettle` loses nothing that matters.** Its doctrine is that **no wage enters the mint**, and
`웃 = M × T` is currency-free too: M is dimensionless, T is hours, no currency until settlement. Its stamped-
rate rule — settlement reads the rate frozen at mint, never a live lookup — is what closed the arbitrage and
is implemented unchanged.
