🔴 **Critical finding pre-fix:** the accord read as if every participant was a healthy urban middle-class typical student. Rural, disabled, neurodivergent, indigenous, first-generation, single-parent, low-connectivity, and gender-diverse participants were implicit at best.

**What Enki changed:**

  - **First-language ≠ regional official language** — every accord artifact available in the participant's native language (Level 3 primitive #25).

  - **Indigenous participants** — cultural + spiritual practices honored; indigenous authority endorsement welcomed in the signature layer; traditional knowledge protected under the confidentiality carve-out where the community requests it.
- Added **"no participant left behind" pledge** — the accord does not measure success by average outcomes; it measures success by the outcome of the most vulnerable Cohort 1 participant.
