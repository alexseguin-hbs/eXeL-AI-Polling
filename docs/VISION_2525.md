# VISION • 2525 — Civilization Framework (reference)

> The "•" between VISION and 2525 is a little Earth/planet. Use **"VISION • 2525"** everywhere it is mentioned.

**Subtitle line used on the launcher:** One Civilization • One Framework • Limitless Possibilities • Humanity's Future

## Core doctrine
- Humanity decides. Technology assists. Trust must be proven.
- Coordination **without domination**; connect awareness, not ownership.
- Three intelligences: **HI** (Human — purpose/ethics/authority), **AI** (capability — pattern/forecast/sim), **SI** (Spiritual — conscience/stewardship/restraint). Also framed as Collective Intelligence turning experience into visible priorities.
- The disciplined ladder: **pilot → replay → qualify → certify → adopt → educate → expand.**

## The 12 Guiding Principles for Humanity (Mastery of Eternal Wisdom)
dignity · truth · wisdom · courage · compassion · accountability · stewardship · resilience · discernment · creativity · unity · evolution
